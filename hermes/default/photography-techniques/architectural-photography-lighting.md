# Architectural Photography Lighting

Lighting strategies for interior and architectural photography.

## Natural Light Optimization

### Golden Hour Exteriors
- **Time**: 30 min after sunrise / before sunset
- **Direction**: Side-lighting for texture, backlighting for silhouette
- **Settings**: f/8-f/11, ISO 100, tripod

### Blue Hour Exteriors
- **Time**: 20-40 min after sunset
- **Balance**: Warm interior lights vs cool ambient sky
- **Settings**: f/5.6-f/8, ISO 100-400, 15-30s exposure

### Interior Daylight
- **Window management**: Diffusion (sheer curtains) for harsh sun
- **Bracketing**: 3-5 exposures (-2, -1, 0, +1, +2 EV) for HDR
- **Flash fill**: Low-power flash for shadow detail

## Artificial Lighting Techniques

### Strobe/Flash for Interiors
- **Key principle**: Light should look like natural window light
- **Placement**: High angle, 45° to camera, outside frame
- **Power**: 1/4-1/2 power for large rooms
- **Modifiers**: Large softbox (60"+) or umbrella with diffusion

### Light Painting (Long Exposure)
- **Use case**: Large spaces, dramatic effect
- **Technique**: 30s+ exposure, walk through with flash
- **Settings**: f/8-f/11, ISO 100, manual flash pops

### Continuous LED
- **Advantage**: WYSIWYG, video + photo
- **Color**: 5600K daylight balanced
- **CRI**: 95+ for accurate materials

## Composition & Perspective

### Vertical Correction
- **In-camera**: Tilt-shift lens or shift adapter
- **Post**: Lightroom Transform → Vertical correction
- **DaVinci Resolve**: Color → Transform → Keystone

### Key Angles
- **Wide establishing**: 16-24mm equivalent
- **Detail/vignette**: 35-50mm equivalent
- **Compression**: 70-100mm for detail stacking

## HDR Workflow

1. **Bracket**: 5 shots, 2EV spacing
2. **Merge**: Lightroom HDR or Photomatix
3. **Tone map**: Natural look, avoid halos
4. **Local adjustment**: Windows pulled down, shadows lifted

## Color Management

- **Calibration**: X-Rite ColorChecker at start of shoot
- **Profile**: Camera-specific DCP profile
- **Delivery**: sRGB for web, Adobe RGB for print
