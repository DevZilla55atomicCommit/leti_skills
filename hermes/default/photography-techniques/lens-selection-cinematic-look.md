# Lens Selection for Cinematic Look

How focal length choices create specific visual storytelling effects.

## Focal Length Characteristics

| Focal Length (FF equiv) | Look | Use Case |
|------------------------|------|----------|
| 14-24mm | Extreme wide, distortion | Tight interiors, dramatic architecture, immersive POV |
| 24-35mm | Wide, environmental | Establishing shots, environmental portraits, walking shots |
| 35mm | "Normal" wide | Documentary style, street, natural perspective |
| 50mm | Standard, human eye | Portraits, interviews, neutral perspective |
| 85mm | Short telephoto | Compressed portraits, subject isolation, beauty |
| 100-135mm | Medium telephoto | Headshots, detail isolation, compressed backgrounds |
| 200mm+ | Long telephoto | Extreme compression, surveillance feel, sports |

## Sensor Size Impact

| Sensor | Crop Factor | 50mm Lens Equivalent |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| Full Frame | 1.0x | 50mm |
| APS-C | 1.5x | 75mm |
| Micro 4/3 | 2.0x | 100mm |
| Super 35 | ~1.4-1.5x | 70-75mm |

**Key insight**: Same lens = different look on different sensors.

## Aperture for Cinematic Depth

| Aperture | Depth of Field | Cinematic Use |
|----------|----------------|---------------|
| f/1.2-f/1.8 | Ultra shallow | Dreamy, isolating, emotional |
| f/2.0-f/2.8 | Shallow | Standard cinematic, subject pop |
| f/4-f/5.6 | Moderate | Environmental context, groups |
| f/8-f/11 | Deep | Architecture, landscape, everything sharp |

## Lens "Character" Factors

- **Bokeh quality**: Circular vs cat-eye, smooth vs nervous
- **Flare characteristics**: Veiling vs structured, color cast
- **Sharpness falloff**: Center vs edge, corner performance
- **Distortion**: Barrel (wide) vs pincushion (tele)
- **Breathing**: Focus shift changes framing (video critical)

## Practical Lens Kits

### Minimal 2-Lens Kit
1. **24-70mm f/2.8** — Versatile zoom (wide to portrait)
2. **70-200mm f/2.8** — Compression, reach

### Prime Kit (Super 35 / APS-C)
1. **16mm f/1.4** — Wide establishing
2. **30mm f/1.4** — Normal documentary
3. **56mm f/1.2** — Portrait isolation

### Anamorphic Look (Budget)
- **Sirui 50mm f/1.8 1.33x** — Oval bokeh, horizontal flare
- **Crop to 2.39:1** in post for scope ratio
