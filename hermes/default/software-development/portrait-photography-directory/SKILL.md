# Portrait Photography & Creative Direction

This skill governs the development of a professional portrait photography resource library, focusing on high-level creative direction rather than just technical settings.

## Workflow: The 4 Learning Pillars
When researching or building resources for a specific genre (e.g., Portraits), the agent should organize content into these four distinct categories:

1. **Fundamentals** (Lighting & Foundation) - *The theory of light/shadow.*
2. **Technical Mastery** (Gear & Settings) - *Specific lens dynamics, AF-system mastering, and depth-of-field control.*
3. **Creative Style** (Artistry & Direction) - *Communication techniques, posing for body diversity, and lifestyle vs. studio transitions.*
4. **Post-Processing** (The Final Polish) - *Frequency separation, color science, and dynamic range management.*

## Specific Guidelines: "Perfect Out of Camera"
For all research tasks involving "getting a perfect shot," prioritize the following when generating data for the user:
- **Focus on Preservation:** Ensure light manipulation techniques focus on keeping the highest possible amount of dynamic range for later color grading.
- **Emphasis on Actionable Director Skills:** When requested, emphasize active verbs and emotional connection (e.g., "active reflection" vs. "static poses").

## Pitfalls
- Avoid suggesting purely beginner-level camera settings without including how to achieve a specific *artistic* look (e.g., don't just say 'set f/2.8', explain how that affects the background blur).
- Do not suggest manual editing tricks as a replacement for correct in-camera exposure foundations.

## Research Instructions
When using `browser_navigate` or `web_search` to find "Professional" techniques, prioritize:
1. Professional photographer blogs (e.g., PetaPixel, individual studio sites).
2. Creative Director manuals/guides.
3. Technical breakdowns of specific lenses (G-Master, etc.).
