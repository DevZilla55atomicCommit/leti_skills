# Portrait & Creative Direction Research Protocol

This skill governs the research and curation of portraiture resources, moving beyond "beginner" guides into high-end creative direction and technical mastery for the TamaZila project.

## The 4 Pillars of Professional Research
When identifying resources or researching new genres, always categorize information into these four buckets:

1.  **Fundamentals (Lighting & Foundation):** The theory of light/shadow (High Key vs Low Key, Golden Hour dynamics).
2.  **Technical Mastery (Gear & Settings):** Specific lens dynamics (e.g., 85mm G-Master) and AF system mastery.
3.  **Creative Style (Artistry & Direction):** Communication techniques for non-professional models, Active Reflection, the "Triangle Rule," and Posing for Body Diversity.
4.  **Post-Processing (The Final Polish):** Color science, frequency separation, and Dynamic Range management.

## Core Standards: "Perfect Out of Camera"
Every research result must prioritize **Data Preservation**. 
- When providing settings to make an image "perfect," the primary goal is retaining dynamic range so that secondary color grading remains seamless.
- Focus on **Active Verbs** in posing (motion) rather than static commands.

## Workflow: Resource Generation
When a user asks for a resource or research:
1.  **Define the Pillar:** Identify which of the 4 pillars the request falls under.
2.  **Synthesis vs. Reference:** Do not just link to tutorials; synthesize the information into actionable "Director" techniques.
3.  **Identify Specifics:** Always provide specific gear-based logic (e.g., how a specific focal length impacts compression).

## Pitfalls
*   Focusing on static poses rather than active movements/directions.
*   Providing technical settings without explaining the artistic *why*.
*   Suggesting adjustments that sacrifice dynamic range for temporary "flat" perfection.
