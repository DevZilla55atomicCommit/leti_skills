title: Log Queue Entry Template
description: Standardized entry format for marking unavailable Instagram URLs in the learning queue.
---

## Log Entry Template

**URL:** `{{URL}}`  
**Status:** ❌ Unavailable  
**Reason:** `{{Reason}}`  
**Comment:** `{{Comment}}`  

**Example:**  
**URL:** `https://www.instagram.com/reel/DL_IgRtnyQo/`  
**Status:** ❌ Unavailable  
**Reason:** Deleted/private – inaccessible due to Instagram removal or privacy settings  
**Comment:** Marked as unavailable and logged per @instagram-unavailable-content-handling skill
