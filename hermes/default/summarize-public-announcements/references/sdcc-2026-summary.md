# SDCC 2026 Marvel Hall H Panel Summary

## Announcements

- **Ghost Rider**: Ryan Gosling joins MCU in solo film directed by Shawn Levy, release 2028.
- **Black Panther 3**: David Jonsson cast as T'Challa II (son of T'Challa), release Dec 15, 2028. Coogler confirmed 70mm shooting.
- **Avengers: Doomsday**: Robert Downey Jr. confirmed as Doctor Doom, ensemble cast includes Hayley Atwell, Chris Evans, Pedro Pascal, Letitia Wright, Winston Duke. Release Dec 18, 2026.
- **VisionQuest**: Disney+ series premieres Oct 14, 2026, third chapter of WandaVision trilogy.

## Footage Revealed

- **Avengers: Doomsday**: Exclusive Hall H footage shows Reed Richards confronting Doom, Sue Storm’s emotional description of Doom, Thor vs. Doom battle.
- **Black Panther 3**: Footage highlights Reed Richards and Victor Von Doom's history, teasing conflict.
- **Punisher & Spider-Man**: New clip shows fight with Hulk; includes dialogue “saving your ass”.
- **Avengers: Endgame Encore**: Trailer debut for re-release on Sept 25, 2026, includes new Doomsday teasers.

## Hall H Moments

- Ryan Reynolds crashes panel in new grey Deadpool suit, jokes with Paul Rudd.
- Doctor Doom masks and cloaks handed to all 6,500 attendees.
- Herbie (Fantastic Four robot) appears on stage.
- Jon Bernthal promotes Spider-Man: Brand New Day (July 31 release).
