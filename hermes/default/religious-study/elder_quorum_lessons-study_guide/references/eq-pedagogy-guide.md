# Elder's Quorum Pedagogy Guide

## Teaching Adult Men in the Restored Church

### Core Principles

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Respect Agency** | Men respond to invitation, not manipulation. "Consider..." not "You must..." |
| **Honor Experience** | EQ members have decades of life experience. Draw it out. |
| **Practical First** | Start with "How does this work in real life?" then go to doctrine. |
| **Brotherhood** | "We're in this together." Shared struggle, shared growth. |
| **Action-Oriented** | Every lesson ends with "What will you DO differently this week?" |

---

## 50-Minute Lesson Structure

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  0:00–0:05  │ Welcome & Opening Prayer (2 min)              │
│  0:05–0:12  │ Hook: Story/Question/Object Lesson (7 min)    │
│  0:12–0:20  │ Doctrine: Talk Segment + Scripture (8 min)    │
│  0:20–0:40  │ Discussion: 3-4 Questions (20 min)           │
│  0:40–0:47  │ Application: Personal Challenge (7 min)       │
│  0:47–0:50  │ Testimony & Closing Prayer (3 min)            │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Timing Guidelines
- **Never** let one person dominate >2 min
- **Always** have a backup question ready
- **Protect** the last 10 min for application
- **End** on time — it's a sign of respect

## Discussion Question Design

### Good EQ Questions
| Type | Example | Why It Works |
|------|---------|--------------|
| **Experience-Based** | "When has paying tithing required real sacrifice for you?" | Honors life experience |
| **Application** | "What's one financial decision you'll make differently this month?" | Leads to action |
| **Doctrine + Life** | "How does 'firstlings' change how you view your paycheck?" | Connects principle to practice |
| **Scripture Wrestling** | "What does 'arubbah = floodgates' mean for your testimony of Malachi 3:10?" | Intellectual engagement |

### Avoid
| Type | Example | Why It Fails |
|------|---------|--------------|
| **Yes/No** | "Do you believe in tithing?" | No discussion |
| **Guess the Answer** | "What did Elder Becerra say about...?" | Feels like a quiz |
| **Overly Personal** | "How much do you tithe?" | Violates privacy |
| **Abstract Only** | "What is the theological significance of...?" | Disconnected from life |

## Discussion Facilitation Techniques

### The "Pass the Baton" Method
1. Ask question
2. Wait 5-7 seconds (silence is productive)
3. Call on someone by name: "Brother [Name], what do you think?"
4. After response: "Who wants to build on that?"
5. Synthesize: "So we're hearing..."

### The "Pair-Share" Method (for sensitive topics)
1. "Turn to the person next to you. 2 minutes each: share an experience..."
2. Walk the room, listen
3. "Any insights from your conversations?"
4. Captures voices that won't speak in large group

### The "Scripture Chain" Method
1. "Let's look at three verses together: Malachi 3:10, 3 Nephi 24:10, D&C 119:6"
2. "What's the same? What's different?"
3. "How does each add to our understanding?"

## Handling Common Situations

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| **Silence after question** | "Take a moment to think. I'll ask again in 10 seconds." / Rephrase question |
| **One brother dominates** | "Thanks, Brother [Name]. Let's hear from someone who hasn't shared yet." |
| **Controversial opinion** | "That's an interesting perspective. What do others think?" / "Let's look at what the talk says..." |
| **Off-topic rabbit trail** | "Great point — let's park that for after class. Back to our question..." |
| **Doctrinal error** | "Let me read what Elder Becerra actually said..." / "The talk references [scripture]..." |
| **Emotional sharing** | "Thank you for sharing that. That takes courage." / Validate, don't fix |

## Elder Becerra Talk — EQ Adaptation Notes

### Hook Options (Choose One)
1. **Object Lesson**: Hold up a car key. "Elder Becerra sold his car to pay tithing. What would you sell?"
2. **Question**: "Raise your hand if you've ever felt the 'awful dilemma' of wanting to serve God but feeling pulled by financial pressure."
3. **Story**: "President Hinckley said people won't walk out of poverty without tithing. Agree or disagree?"

### Key Discussion Questions (Pick 3-4)

1. **Experience**: "Elder Becerra describes tithing as resolving the 'awful dilemma' of double-mindedness. When has a concrete act of obedience brought you spiritual clarity?"
   
2. **Doctrine + Life**: "The talk teaches Adam's firstlings were a 'similitude of Christ.' How does understanding this change tithing from 'paying a bill' to 'offering a similitude'?"

3. **Scripture Wrestling**: "Elder Bednar says Malachi's 'windows' (arubbah) are floodgates, not trickles. What 'floodgates' have opened in your life through tithing?"

4. **Application**: "Elder Becerra's mother testified: 'All we need to see is the very hand of Jehovah.' How do we recognize God's hand in our tithing blessings?"

5. **Priorities**: "Matthew 6:33 says 'seek first the kingdom.' What competes for 'first place' in your life, and how does tithing recalibrate?"

6. **Faith Story**: "Elder Becerra sold his car, then the Lord provided another way. Share a time the Lord provided an unexpected 'car' after you put Him first."

### Application Challenge (Last 7 Minutes)
```
"Brothers, here's your challenge for the week:

1. LOOK at your budget — is tithing truly FIRST, or what's left over?
2. IDENTIFY one area of double-mindedness — commit to decisive action
3. OFFER a 'firstlings' sacrifice — time, talent, or means given BEFORE anything else
4. RECORD the blessings that follow — recognize the hand of Jehovah
5. SHARE your experience with your family or quorum next Sunday

Write it down. Do it. Report back."
```

## Visual Aids for EQ

| Aid | Purpose | Implementation |
|-----|---------|----------------|
| **Pocket Card** | 4×6 reference during lesson | Key scriptures, 3 questions, challenge steps |
| **Handout** | 1-page take-home | Core message, scriptures, challenge |
| **Whiteboard** | Track discussion threads | "Double-mindedness" ← "Tithing" → "Windows" |
| **Slide** | Only if needed | Talk title, key verse, challenge |

### What NOT to Use
- ❌ Long PowerPoints (loses engagement)
- ❌ Videos >3 min (passive consumption)
- ❌ Handouts >1 page (won't be read)

## Teacher Preparation Checklist

### Sunday Morning (30 min)
- [ ] Read talk again (15 min)
- [ ] Select 3 discussion questions (5 min)
- [ ] Prepare pocket card/handout copies (5 min)
- [ ] Pray for the brethren by name (5 min)

### During Week (Optional Deepening)
- [ ] Read 1 related GC talk
- [ ] Read 1 Gospel Topics essay
- [ ] Listen to 1 podcast segment
- [ ] Prepare personal experience to share

### Mental Preparation
- [ ] "I am a facilitator, not a lecturer"
- [ ] "The Spirit teaches through their words too"
- [ ] "Every brother has something to contribute"
- [ ] "My testimony is the capstone, not the foundation"

## Post-Lesson Reflection

After class, note:
1. **What worked?** (Question, method, moment)
2. **What fell flat?** (Question, timing, dynamic)
3. **Who didn't speak?** (Reach out individually)
4. **What did the Spirit highlight?** (For next time)
