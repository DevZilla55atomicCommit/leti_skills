# Podcast Source List for Come Follow Me Study Guides

## Primary Sources (Current 2026 Season)

### 1. followHIM Podcast
**Primary source** — Expert guests, Hebrew insights, current season
- **Website**: https://followhim.co/
- **YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/@followHIM
- **Old Testament 2026**: https://followhim.co/old-testament-2026
- **Format**: Part 1 (60-80 min) + Part 2 (45-60 min)
- **Guests**: Professors, scholars, General Authorities
- **Strengths**: Hebrew word studies, historical context, Restoration connections

**Key Guests for 2026**:
- Dr. Marcus Martins (Job) — Temple journey, Go'el, daughters' names
- Steven Sharp Nelson (Psalms) — Music, creativity, Psalms as "People's Hymnbook"
- Dr. Shon Hopkin (previous seasons) — Psalm 22, music in worship

### 2. Scripture Insights
**Structural analysis** — Taylor Halverson & Mike Harris
- **YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/@ScriptureInsights
- **Format**: 30-60 min per episode, often multi-part (up to 38 chapters for Psalms!)
- **Strengths**: 5-part Psalms structure, temple themes, literary analysis, Psalms 1-2 as "trailer"
- **Key Episodes**: Psalms 1-48 (multi-part), Isaiah, Temple worship

### 3. Don't Miss This
**Practical application** — Dave & Cali Black
- **YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/@DontMissThis
- **Format**: ~55 min, conversational
- **Strengths**: "Playlist for every season" metaphor, personal application, trust metaphors
- **Key Themes**: Psalm 8 (creation), Psalm 18 (rock/fortress), Psalm 25 (trust in unknown)

### 4. Certain Women Podcast
**Women's perspectives** — Becky Squire & Tess Frame
- **YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/@CertainWomenPodcast
- **Format**: 20 chapters per episode
- **Strengths**: Women's voices in Psalms, shepherd/sheep relationship, rod/staff/oil symbolism, grace perspective ("Shepherd is not grading your performance")

### 5. Latter-Day Insight
**Thematic acts** — Structured 5-act approach
- **YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/@LatterDayInsight
- **Format**: 9 chapters per episode
- **Strengths**: 5-act structure (Divine Shepherd → Messiah → Divine Elevation → Covenants/Purity → Order from Chaos), Psalm 46 focus

### 6. Unshaken
**Psalms for crisis** — Grief, disorientation, when prose fails
- **YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/@Unshaken
- **Format**: 20 chapters
- **Strengths**: Psalms as prayer language for grief/disorientation

### 7. Brother Miller's Notes
**Doctrinal focus** — Psalms quoted most by NT authors
- **YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/@BrotherMillersNotes
- **Format**: ~25 min
- **Strengths**: Psalms quoted most by NT authors, power of music, hymnal perspective

### 8. Line Upon Line
**All-ages overview** — Membership model
- **YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/@LineUponLine
- **Format**: Overview for all ages

### 9. Scripture Central / Faith Insights
**Scholarly** — Tyler Griffin, Scripture Central
- **YouTube**: https://www.youtube.com/@ScriptureCentral
- **Strengths**: Academic approach, Book of Job transformation themes

## Episode Evaluation Checklist
When reviewing a podcast for inclusion:

- [ ] **Current season** (2026) or highly relevant archive
- [ ] **Guest expertise** (professor, scholar, General Authority)
- [ ] **Hebrew/Greek insights** (word studies, literary structure)
- [ ] **Restoration connections** (Book of Mormon, D&C, temple)
- [ ] **Practical application** (not just academic)
- [ ] **Unique angle** (music, women's voices, structural analysis, grace)
- [ ] **Timestamp/chapter list** available for quick navigation
- [ ] **Show notes PDF** available for download
