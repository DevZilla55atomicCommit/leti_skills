# Tithing with No Income — Official churchofjesuschrist.org Research

## Summary
Research conducted to answer: "What if I don't have a job and nothing to sell — do I still need to pay tithing, and how?"

## Key Findings from Official Sources

### 1. D&C 119:4 Definition — "Increase" = Income/Profit/Compensation
- **Source**: [Teachings of Presidents: Howard W. Hunter, Ch. 9](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/teachings-of-presidents-of-the-church-howard-w-hunter/chapter-9-the-law-of-tithing?lang=eng)
- **Quote**: "Interest means profit, compensation, increase. It is the wage of one employed, the profit from the operation of a business, the increase of one who grows or produces, or the income to a person from any other source."
- **Implication**: No income = no increase = no tithing obligation. 10% of $0 = $0.

### 2. Elder Earl C. Tingey — "How Can I Pay Tithing When I Have No Income?"
- **Source**: [Liahona, July 2002 — The Law of Tithing](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/liahona/2002/07/the-law-of-tithing?lang=eng)
- **Direct quote**: Elder Tingey taught a group of Church leaders in Africa. One brother asked: "Elder Tingey, how can I pay tithing when I have no income?" He had a large family, was unemployed. He had a garden and geese. Elder Tingey asked about the geese eggs. The brother realized: "I could take one egg and give it to my branch president."
- **Principle**: Tithing applies to **whatever increase you actually have** — eggs, garden produce, goods, not just cash.

### 3. General Handbook 22.3.2 — Church Assistance for Temporal Needs
- **Source**: [General Handbook 22 — Providing for Temporal Needs](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/22-providing-for-temporal-needs?lang=eng)
- **Quote**: "Members strive to meet their basic needs through their own efforts and help from extended family. When this is insufficient, members may need help from other sources, such as: Government and community resources. Church assistance. Talk to your local Church leaders or bishop to see what resources are available."
- **Action**: If truly no income, talk to bishop — fast offerings/welfare exist for this.

### 4. Personal Story — "Prompted to Pay Tithing" (Ensign Aug 2017)
- **Source**: [Ensign 2017](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/ensign/2017/08/prompted-to-pay-tithing?lang=eng)
- **Scenario**: Author had no job, almost no money, rent due. Felt prompted to pay tithing on meager funds. Bishop gave blessing. Miracle followed (temp job + sale of equipment).
- **Note**: This is a faith testimony, not doctrinal instruction for zero-income situations.

### 5. Widow's Mite Principle (Mark 12:41–44)
- **Source**: Cited in multiple General Conference talks
- **Principle**: It's the **sacrifice/faith**, not the amount. A full tithe payer pays 10% of *their* increase — whether that's a widow's mite or a king's ransom.

## Answer to User's Question
**If you have zero income/increase, you pay zero tithing.** You cannot pay what you don't have. The obligation is 10% of your actual increase. If increase = $0, tithing = $0.

**What to do instead**: Talk to your bishop. The Church's welfare system (fast offerings, bishop's storehouse) is specifically for members in this situation. General Handbook 22.3.2 directs members to "Talk to your local Church leaders or bishop to see what resources are available."
