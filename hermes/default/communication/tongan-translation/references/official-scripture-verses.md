# Official Church Tongan Scripture Quotations — Complete Set
# All verified verbatim from churchofjesuschrist.org (lang=ton)
# These map to every scripture quoted in the Psalms lesson (Aug 17-23, 2026)

## The Lord Is My Shepherd (Psalm 23:1) — subtitle & Q4
EN: "The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want."
TO: KO hoku tauhi a Jihova; e ikai teu majiva.

## Psalm 23:2 (green pastures / still waters)
EN: "He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters."
TO: Oku ne fakatokoto au i he gaahi goue lau mauiui: oku ne tataki au ki he vee vai oku tafetafemalie.

## Psalm 23:3 (restore soul / paths of righteousness)
EN: "He restoreth my soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness."
TO: Oku ne fakamoui hoku laumalie: oku ne tataki au i he gaahi hala oe maonioni koeuhi ko hono huafa.

## Psalm 23:4 (valley / rod and staff)
EN: "Though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death... thy rod and thy staff they comfort me."
TO: Io, ne ogo eku eveeva i he telea oe malumalu oe mate, e ikai teu manavahe ki ha kovi: he oku ke iate au; ko hoo vaa akau mo ho tokotoko oku na fakafiemalie'i au.

## Psalm 23:5 (table / anoint head)
EN: "Thou preparest a table before me... thou anointest my head with oil."
TO: Oku ke teuteu ae keinagaaga maaku i he ao o hoku gaahi fili: oku ke bani aki hoku ulu ae lolo; oku fonu mahuohua a eku ibu.

## Psalm 23:6 (goodness and mercy / dwell forever)
EN: "Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me... and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever."
TO: Koe mooni e muimui iate au ae lelei moe aloofa i he aho kotoabe o eku moui: bea teu nofo i he fale o Jihova o lauikuoga.

## Psalm 27:1 (The Lord is my light) — Q2 Words for God
EN: "The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear?"
TO: Ko eku mama mo hoku fakamoui a Jihova; teu manavahe ki ahai? koe malohi o eku moui a Jihova; bea teu manavahe ki ahai?

## Psalm 46:1 (God is our refuge) — Q1/Q2
EN: "God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble."
TO: Koe Otua ko ho tau hufaga moe malohi, koe tokoni lahi bea ofi i he mamahi.

## Psalm 46:10 (Be still and know) — Q1, cheat
EN: "Be still, and know that I am God."
TO: Mou logo be, bea ilo ko au koe Otua: teu hakeaki'i au i he lotolotoga oe hiteni, teu hakeaki'i au i mamani.

## Psalm 28:1 (Unto thee will I cry) — Q2 Words for God
EN: "Unto thee will I cry, O Lord my rock."
TO: TEU tagi kiate koe, E Jihova ko hoku makatuu; oua naa ke tae afio'i au.

## Psalm 25:1 (lift up my soul) — Don't Miss This
EN: "Unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul."
TO: E JIHOVA, oku ou hiki hake hoku laumalie kiate koe.

## Psalm 22:1 (My God, my God) — Q5, Cross
EN: "My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?"
TO: E HOKU Otua, E hoku Otua, koeha kuo ke liaki ai au? koeha oku ke mamao ai mei he tokoni kiate au.

## Psalm 2:7 (Thou art my Son) — Q5, Messiah
EN: "Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee."
TO: Nae behe e Jihova kiate au, Ko hoku Alo koe; kuou fakatubu koe i he aho ni.

## Psalm 24:1 (The earth is the Lord's) — temple theme
EN: "The earth is the Lord's, and the fulness thereof."
TO: Oku o Jihova ae fonua kotoabe, mo hono mahu oia; a mamani, mo kinautolu oku nofo i ai.

## Psalm 8:1 (name excellent) — creation
EN: "O Lord our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth!"
TO: E JIHOVA ko ho mau Eiki, oku lelei lahi ho huafa i mamani kotoabe!

## Psalm 19:1 (heavens declare) — Q6
EN: "The heavens declare the glory of God."
TO: Oku fakaha e he gaahi lagi ae naunau oe Otua.

## Psalm 2:1 (why do the heathen rage) — Messiah
EN: "Why do the heathen rage?"
TO: Koeha oku matalili ai ae hiteni?

## Orthography note
This uses the official Church Tongan orthography (older Anglican/Free Wesleyan
style): b=p, v/w interchangeable, no macrons in these printed extracts.
For teaching prose, the Lea Faka-Tonga modern orthography is preferred
(macrons, p instead of b). Verify consistency before final print.
