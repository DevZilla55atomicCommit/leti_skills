## Note on spelling variants
The official Church Tongan here uses older orthography:
- **b** for **p** (e.g. "kotoabe" = kotoa pe, "teu nofo" = te u nofo)
- **v** where modern uses **w/v** interchangeable
- no stress accents, some macrons dropped
Modern Church Tongan and the Lea Faka-Tonga course use **p** and standard
macrons (e.g. 'pē', 'pe'). For a Sunday School teaching document, prefer the
modern orthography used by the course while keeping official scripture
quotations accurate. Confirm before mixing orthographies in one document.
