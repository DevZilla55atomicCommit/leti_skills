## User Preference Summary
- **Draft‑first, send‑never** – collaborate on drafts before sending.
- **Concise, direct answers** – avoid verbose tables, long explanations, or exploratory prose.
- **Bullet‑point key takeaways** – summarize core points up front as a short list.
- **Actionable next steps** – focus on clear next moves rather than procedural fluff.
- **Avoid verbose tables** – present information inline or as simple bullet lists when possible.

## Recommended Explanation Structure
1. **TL;DR Summary** – 1‑2 sentence bullet list of the main points.
2. **Context / Findings** – brief sentence(s) of what was discovered.
3. **Implication / Recommendation** – concise action or suggestion.
4. **Next Step** – explicit, single next action (e.g., “draft email reply”, “poll tenants”, “check lock rates”).
