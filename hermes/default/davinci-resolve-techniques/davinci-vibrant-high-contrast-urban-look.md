# Vibrant High-Contrast Urban Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `ClCfduEAfXt` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Vibrant High-Contrast Urban Look](ClCfduEAfXt.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Wheels
- Curves
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.2
- **pivot**: 0.5
- **saturation**: 145
- **lift_offset**: -0.02
- **gamma_tint**: Cyan/Teal

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Lift the blacks slightly to create faded, cinematic blacks.
2. Increase contrast using the Contrast wheel to pop the subject from the background.
3. Use Hue/Saturation curves to boost specifically the pinks and blues in the background murals.
4. Apply a slight blue/cyan tint to the shadows and a warm tint to the highlights.
5. Add a soft vignette to focus attention on the subject in the center.
