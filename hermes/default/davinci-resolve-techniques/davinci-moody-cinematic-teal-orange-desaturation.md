# Moody Cinematic Teal & Orange Desaturation

**Source:** Instagram Reel `DD9tcjiiAqJ` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Moody Cinematic Teal & Orange Desaturation](DD9tcjiiAqJ.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Wheels
- HSL Qualifier
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: +0.15
- **Saturation**: -15.0
- **Lift**: Teal tint
- **Midtones**: Warm/Orange
- **Highlights**: Soft White

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust contrast and pivot to establish a deep base
2. Use the Lift wheel to push shadows toward a teal/blue hue
3. Use the Midtones wheel to add warm/orange tones to skin areas
4. Apply an HSL qualifier to isolate skin tones and protect their saturation/hue
5. Add a soft vignette using a Power Window to draw focus to the subject
6. Reduce global saturation slightly for a film-like look
