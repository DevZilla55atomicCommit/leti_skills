# Blue Hour Teal & Orange Cinematic Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C87VUvIsa1B` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Blue Hour Teal & Orange Cinematic Contrast](C87VUvIsa1B.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- Curves
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **Temperature**: -15.0
- **Tint**: +5.0
- **Contrast**: 1.20
- **Gamma**: 0.95
- **Lift**: -0.010

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust White Balance towards the blue to enhance the cold, blue hour atmosphere.
2. Use the Color Wheels to push shadows toward a deep teal/cyan and highlights toward a subtle warm neutral.
3. Apply a slight S-curve in the Curves tool to deepen the blacks.
4. Use a soft circular vignette to focus attention on the central screen content.
5. Add a slight Grain effect to simulate the digital sensor noise of the drone footage.
