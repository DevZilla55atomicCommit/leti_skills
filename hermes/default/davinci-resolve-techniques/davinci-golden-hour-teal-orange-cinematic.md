# Golden Hour Teal & Orange Cinematic

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8rodOGCzf7` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Golden Hour Teal & Orange Cinematic](C8rodOGCzf7.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Wheels
- Hue vs Saturation
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **lift**: -0.02
- **gamma**: 0.05
- **gain**: 0.1
- **teal_shift**: 210 degrees
- **orange_shift**: 30 degrees

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Lift the black point slightly to fade the shadows for a matte look.
2. Use the Color Wheels to push shadows toward teal/blue.
3. Use the Gain wheel to push highlights toward orange/gold.
4. Use Hue vs Saturation to increase saturation in the skin tones while desaturating the greens/blues.
5. Apply a soft vignette to draw focus to the center subject.
6. Add a slight glow effect in the Fusion tab to soften the sunset highlights.
