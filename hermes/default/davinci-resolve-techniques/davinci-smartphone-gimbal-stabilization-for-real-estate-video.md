## Node Graph Structure

- Clip
- Transform
- Stabilizer

## Parameters

- **stabilizer_mode**: Perspective
- **stabilizer_smoothness**: Medium
- **stabilizer_crop_amount**: Automatic

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Import your smartphone footage into DaVinci Resolve.
2. Place the clip on the timeline in the Edit page.
3. Select the clip and navigate to the Inspector panel.
4. Go to the 'Transform' tab and enable 'Stabilizer'.
5. Choose a 'Mode' (e.g., 'Perspective' for smooth camera movements).
6. Adjust 'Smoothness' to control the level of stabilization.
7. Let DaVinci Resolve analyze the footage.
8. Review the stabilized footage and adjust 'Crop Amount' if necessary to avoid unwanted black borders.
