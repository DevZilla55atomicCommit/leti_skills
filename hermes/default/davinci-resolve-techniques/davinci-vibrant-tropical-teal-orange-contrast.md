# Vibrant Tropical Teal & Orange Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C5VoZA0vdGK` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Vibrant Tropical Teal & Orange Contrast](C5VoZA0vdGK.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Curves Contrast
- Color Wheels
- Qualifier Masking

## Parameters

- **Saturation**: 65+
- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Midtone Tint**: Yellow/Orange
- **Shadows Tint**: Teal/Blue
- **Highlights Tint**: Warm/Yellow

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure to balance the bright sky without clipping skin tones.
2. Increase contrast using the S-curve in the Curves panel for depth.
3. Use the Saturation slider to boost the vibrancy of the yellow dress and blue water.
4. In the Color Wheels, push shadows and blues slightly toward a teal/cyan hue.
5. Use a Qualifier to select the yellow dress and slightly increase its saturation and luminance specifically.
6. Apply a slight glow or bloom effect to the highlights to mimic the dreamy tropical look.
