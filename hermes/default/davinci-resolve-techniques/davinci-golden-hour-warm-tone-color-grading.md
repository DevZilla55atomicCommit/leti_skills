# Golden Hour Warm Tone Color Grading

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C22bFUxr8xE` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Golden Hour Warm Tone Color Grading](C22bFUxr8xE.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Color Corrector
- LUT

## Parameters

- **white_balance**: warm
- **saturation**: slightly increased

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Import footage into DaVinci Resolve
2. Add Color Corrector node in serial workflow
3. Adjust white balance to golden hour tones (e.g., shift towards orange/yellow)
4. Apply a warm LUT for consistent golden hour aesthetic
