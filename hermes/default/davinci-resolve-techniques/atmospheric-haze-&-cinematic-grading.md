# Atmospheric Haze & Cinematic Grading

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C_s_3BrqhAl`
**Resolve Page:** Color
**Difficulty:** intermediate
**Node Graph Type:** serial

## Overview
DaVinci Resolve technique extracted from Instagram Reel analysis.

## Key Nodes
- Primary Correction
- Power Window
- Color Mixer

## Parameters
- **Contrast:** Lowered
- **Saturation:** Reduced
- **Midtone Detail:** Increased
- **Tint:** Teal/Blue shift

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Add a primary node to balance exposure and lower the overall contrast
2. Apply a Power Window to the landmass to slightly increase saturation locally
3. Use the Glow effect or a slight Blur node to create a misty/atmospheric look
4. Shift shadows toward blue/teal and highlights toward a warm or neutral tone using Color Wheels
5. Adjust the Midtone Detail to bring back texture to the water and trees
