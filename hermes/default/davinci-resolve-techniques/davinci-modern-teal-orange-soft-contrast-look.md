# Modern Teal & Orange Soft Contrast Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `Cj6hJ7fjXyJ` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Modern Teal & Orange Soft Contrast Look](Cj6hJ7fjXyJ.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- Curves
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.20
- **saturation**: 0.45
- **midtone_tint**: Warm/Amber
- **shadow_tint**: Teal/Blue
- **pivot**: 0.55

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust Exposure and Balance to neutralize whites in the kitchen environment
2. Use Color Wheels to push Shadows toward a subtle teal/blue hue
3. Push Midtones and Highlights toward a warm amber/orange for skin tone protection
4. Apply a slight S-curve in the Curves tool to add cinematic contrast
5. Desaturate overall image slightly to achieve a clean lifestyle aesthetic
6. Add a soft vignette to draw focus to the center of the frame
