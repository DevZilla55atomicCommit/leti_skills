# Warm Cinematic Contrast & Depth Focus

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C4Dz-FLoEb8` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Warm Cinematic Contrast & Depth Focus](C4Dz-FLoEb8.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- Qualifier
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Temperature**: 5500
- **Saturation**: 65
- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Midtone-Gamma**: +0.05

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust White Balance to favor a warm, golden-toned highlight.
2. Use the Contrast tool to increase depth while lifting the blacks slightly for a film look.
3. Use a Qualifier to select the yellow elements (case/hat) and boost their saturation specifically.
4. Apply a soft Power Window around the subject to slightly increase exposure and draw focus to the center.
5. Add a subtle vignette to focus attention on the action in the POV and Close-up shots.
