# Moody Warm-Teal Interior Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C3QqulutpON` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Moody Warm-Teal Interior Contrast](C3QqulutpON.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Parallel Qualifier
- Color Wheels
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.2
- **pivot**: 0.45
- **shadow_tint**: Teal/Blue
- **highlight_tint**: Amber/Gold
- **saturation**: 0.65

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Increase contrast and drop the pivot to deepen the shadows without losing highlight detail.
2. Use the Lift wheel to push shadows toward a deep teal or navy blue hue.
3. Use the Gain wheel to push highlights toward a warm orange/gold tone.
4. Apply a Power Window or Qualifier to desaturate the mid-tones slightly, keeping the warm tones of the leather and light-glow saturated.
5. Add a soft vignette to darken the edges of the frame and draw attention to the center espresso machine/chair area.
6. Add a slight green-blue tint to the shadows using the Color Wheels.
