# Warm Golden Hour Soft-Contrast Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `CnkJIWXqXFW` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Warm Golden Hour Soft-Contrast Look](CnkJIWXqXFW.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Temperature**: +5.0
- **Tint**: +2.0
- **Contrast**: 0.45
- **Pivot**: 0.55
- **Lift**: Lifted (faded blacks)
- **Gain**: Warm/Orange tint

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust Temperature toward yellow/orange to establish a warm base tone.
2. Use the Lift wheel to slightly raise the shadows, fading the blacks for a cinematic matte look.
3. Use the Curves tool to create a subtle S-curve, but keep the midtones soft.
4. In the Primary Color Wheels, add a small amount of orange to the Highlights and Midtones.
5. Apply a Power Window around the subject to slightly increase exposure and saturation locally, making her pop.
6. Add a slight glow effect using the Blur tab to soften the highlights on the skin tones.
