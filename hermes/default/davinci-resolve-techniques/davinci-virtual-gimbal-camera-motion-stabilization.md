# Virtual Gimbal / Camera Motion Stabilization

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C4Mj9SYPIAM` (Gimbal_Moves)  
**Page:** Edit | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Virtual Gimbal / Camera Motion Stabilization](C4Mj9SYPIAM.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Inspector > Stabilizer

## Parameters

- **Mode**: Perspective
- **Smoothing**: 50.0
- **Camera Lock**: Off (unless desired)

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Place the clip on the Edit page timeline
2. Open the Inspector panel
3. Navigate to the Stabilizer tab
4. Set Mode to Perspective for architectural shots
5. Click Stabilize to remove jitter
6. Adjust the Smoothing value to maintain natural movement
7. Use Camera Motion to simulate smooth dolly or pan movements if needed
