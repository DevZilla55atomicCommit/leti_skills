**Source:** Instagram Reel `DKfcWLTs1iV`

## Parameters

- **exposure_monitoring:** Zebras
- **focus_assist:** Contrast Focus

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Connect camera HDMI output to an external monitor (like Atomos)
2. Enable the 3x3 grid overlay on the monitor settings
3. Mount the monitor on the camera rig for stability and better visibility
4. Use the monitor to perform precise focus and exposure checks independent of the camera LCD
