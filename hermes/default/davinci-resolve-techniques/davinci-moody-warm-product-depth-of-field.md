- **vignette_intensity**: 0.4
- **grain_amount**: 0.15

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use a shallow depth of field in-production or apply Gaussian Blur to isolate the subject.
2. Lift the black point slightly in the Curves tool to create matte/faded shadows.
3. Increase the temperature slider in the Gain wheels to add a warm, golden amber glow.
4. Reduce overall saturation to maintain a premium, desaturated aesthetic.
5. Apply a soft vignette to draw focus to the sharp center of the frame.
6. Add a fine layer of film grain to simulate a cinematic camera stock look.
