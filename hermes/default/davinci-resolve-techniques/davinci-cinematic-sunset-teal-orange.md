# Cinematic Sunset Teal & Orange

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8jFhFVINQ-` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Sunset Teal & Orange](C8jFhFVINQ-.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- Hue Saturation Curves
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **Lift**: 0
- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Shadows Tint**: Blue/Cyan
- **Highlights Tint**: Orange/Pink
- **Saturation**: 65
- **Midtone Detail**: +15

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure and contrast to recover detail in highlights while maintaining silhouettes.
2. Use the lift wheel to push shadows toward a cool blue/teal tone.
3. Use the gain wheel to push highlights toward a warm orange/magenta tone.
4. Use Hue vs Saturation curves to specifically target and increase the saturation of the oranges and purples.
5. Apply a soft vignette to draw focus to the center camera screen and subjects.
