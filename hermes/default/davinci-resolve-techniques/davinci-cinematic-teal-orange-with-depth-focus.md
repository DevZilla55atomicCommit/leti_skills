# Cinematic Teal & Orange with Depth Focus

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9Kk_CiKjSI` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Teal & Orange with Depth Focus](C9Kk_CiKjSI.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Windows (Masking)
- Color Wheels
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **saturation**: 0.65
- **contrast**: 1.2
- **pivot**: 0.55
- **shadow_tint**: Teal/Blue
- **highlight_tint**: Orange/Warm

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure and balance using Primary Wheels to establish a moody base.
2. Use a Power Window or circular mask to isolate the subject and slightly increase exposure and saturation.
3. In the Color Wheels, push shadows toward a teal hue and highlights toward a warm orange.
4. Apply a vignette to draw focus to the center of the frame.
5. Use the Qualifier tool to ensure skin tones remain natural while grading the background shifts.
6. Add a slight film grain effect in the Effects tab for a filmic texture.
