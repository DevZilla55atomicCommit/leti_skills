# Ethereal Soft Diffusion & Moody Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8q-DbcRTi1` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

*No preview GIF available*

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Contrast Balance
- Glow/Diffusion
- Warm Highlights Overlay

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.4
- **pivot**: 0.45
- **lift**: 0.02
- **glow_size**: 60.0
- **glow_threshold**: 0.5
- **warm_tint_offset**: +15

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Lift the blacks slightly to fade the shadows for a film-like look.
2. Increase contrast and adjust pivot to pop the white ceramics against the dark wall.
3. Apply a Glow node with a high threshold and large size to simulate a pro-mist diffusion filter.
4. Use a Power Window with a warm tint (orange/yellow) on the left side to simulate the light flare.
5. Desaturate the overall image slightly while keeping the wood tones vibrant.
