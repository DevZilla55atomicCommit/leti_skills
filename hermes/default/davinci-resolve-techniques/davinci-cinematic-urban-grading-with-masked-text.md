# Cinematic Urban Grading with Masked Text

**Source:** Instagram Reel `DKkNZCcusUx` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Urban Grading with Masked Text](DKkNZCcusUx.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Balance
- Power Window
- Text+ Tracker

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: High
- **Saturation**: Boosted slightly
- **Temperature**: Warm (Orange highlights)
- **Vignette**: Soft edges

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Import clip and go to the Color page to adjust overall exposure and contrast.
2. Add a serial node for Color Balance to push warm tones into highlights and cool tones into shadows.
3. Use a Power Window or Qualifier to slightly isolate the subject and increase their exposure/mid.
4. Go to the Fusion page or use the Text+ tool in Edit page.
5. Apply a Tracker to the text layer to ensure it follows the movement of the subject or camera.
6. Adjust the opacity and shadow of the text to blend with the urban environment.
