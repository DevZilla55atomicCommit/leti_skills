# Horizontal Split-Screen Comparison

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C3ta12bJ3S8` (Gimbal_Moves)  
**Page:** Edit | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Horizontal Split-Screen Comparison](C3ta12bJ3S8.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Video Clip 1
- Video Clip 2

## Parameters

- **Crop Right**: 50
- **Position Y**: 0.5
- **Opacity**: 100

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Place the two video clips on the timeline, one directly above the other.
2. Select the top clip and go to the Inspector tab.
3. Adjust the 'Crop Right' slider to 50% to cut the clip in half.
4. Adjust the 'Position Y' or 'Zoom' to align the desired framing within the remaining half.
5. Add a Solid Color or a Transition between the clips to create a clean divider line.
