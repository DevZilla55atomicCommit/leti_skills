# Clean Cinematic Teal-Orange Warm Skin Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C3m9FQaOT1u` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Clean Cinematic Teal-Orange Warm Skin Grade](C3m9FQaOT1u.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Skin Tone
- Color Balance
- Power Windows

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: +0.15
- **Pivot**: 0.45
- **Saturation**: 520
- **Midtone Tint**: Warm/Orange
- **Shadow Tint**: Teal/Blue

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight S-curve to deepen blacks and pop whites.
2. Use a Power Window on the subject to slightly increase exposure and sharpness locally.
3. Use the Qualifier to isolate skin tones and push them toward orange on the hue wheel.
4. In the Color Wheels, push shadows toward a teal and highlights toward a warm yellow/orange.
5. Add a subtle vignette to focus attention on the center frame.
