# High-Contrast Minimalist Pop Color

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C_JKZNtN7X3` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![High-Contrast Minimalist Pop Color](C_JKZNtN7X3.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Wheels
- Curves
- Color Qualifier
- Saturation

## Parameters

- **contrast**: +25.0
- **saturation**: -30.0
- **pivot**: 0.5
- **blacks_lift**: -0.02

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use Primary Wheels to desaturate the overall image and lift the blacks to ensure a clean matte look.
2. Increase contrast via the Curves node to sharpen the checkered floor patterns.
3. Use a Color Qualifier or Power Mask to select only the blue tones of the subject shirt.
4. Increase the saturation specifically on the selected blue area to make it pop against the monochromatic background.
5. Apply a slight cyan tint to the highlights to maintain a cold, modern architectural feel.
