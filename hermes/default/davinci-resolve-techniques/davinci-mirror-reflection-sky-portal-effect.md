## Parameters

- **cameraAngle**: Top-down
- **mirrorPlacement**: Flat ground
- **maskFeather**: Soft edge

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Place a flat mirror on the ground in an open area
2. Position the camera at a high angle (drone or crane) looking directly down
3. Film the subject interacting with the mirror to capture the sky reflection
4. In DaVinci Resolve, use a mask to isolate the mirror area if needed for color grading
5. Color grade the reflection separately to enhance the vibrancy of the sky tones
