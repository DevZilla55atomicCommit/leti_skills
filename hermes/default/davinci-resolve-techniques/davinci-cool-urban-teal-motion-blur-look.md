# Cool Urban Teal Motion Blur Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C7_UoMpS338` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cool Urban Teal Motion Blur Look](C7_UoMpS338.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Wheels
- Power Window
- Motion Blur Effect

## Parameters

- **temperature**: 6500
- **tint**: -5
- **shadows_tint**: Teal/Blue
- **saturation**: 35.0
- **contrast**: 1.2

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use the Primary wheels to balance exposure, applying a slight lift to the blacks.
2. Use Color Wheels to push shadows toward teal and midtones toward a cool blue/grey.
3. Use the Saturation slider to desaturate the overall image for an industrial feel.
4. Apply a Power Window on the train body to slightly increase sharpness and contrast relative to the background.
5. If the footage is static, use the 'Motion Blur' tool in the Fusion page to simulate the high-speed movement.
