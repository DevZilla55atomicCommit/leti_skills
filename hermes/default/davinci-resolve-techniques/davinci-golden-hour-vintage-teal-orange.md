# Golden Hour Vintage Teal & Orange

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8j_6oFpLpI` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Golden Hour Vintage Teal & Orange](C8j_6oFpLpI.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- Color Wheels
- Custom Curves
- Qualifier
- Film Grain

## Parameters

- **temperature**: +500
- **tint**: -2.0
- **lift_offset**: 0.02
- **gain_boost**: 1.15
- **saturation**: 1.2

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure and balance to preserve highlights in the sky and car.
2. Use Color Wheels to add a warm/orange tint to the highlights and a subtle teal/blue tint to the shadows.
3. Apply a S-curve in the Custom Curves to increase contrast, lifting the black point slightly for a faded look.
4. Use a Qualifier to isolate skin tones and slightly increase saturation and luminance to make them pop.
5. Add a subtle Film Grain overlay to simulate a cinematic film aesthetic.
