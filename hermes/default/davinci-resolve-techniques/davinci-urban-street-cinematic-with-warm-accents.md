# Urban Street Cinematic with Warm Accents

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9P8m-Zp2va` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Urban Street Cinematic with Warm Accents](C9P8m-Zp2va.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- HSL Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Windows

## Parameters

- **Saturation**: +15
- **Contrast**: +0.1
- **Lift**: Slightly raised
- **Midtones**: Warm/Orange
- **Highlights**: Teal/Blue

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure and white balance in Primaries to normalize daylight
2. Use HSL curves to selectively boost the reds in the brick and the blues in the shirt
3. Apply a Power Window to the background globe lights to increase exposure and warmth slightly
4. Lift the blacks slightly to create a faded cinematic shadow look
5. Add a subtle teal-and-orange color grade using the Color Wheels for color contrast
