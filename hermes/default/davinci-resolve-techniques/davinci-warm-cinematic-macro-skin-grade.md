# Warm Cinematic Macro Skin Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9KaI5fRuq5` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Warm Cinematic Macro Skin Grade](C9KaI5fRuq5.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure Correction
- Primary Balance
- HSL Qualifier
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **Temperature**: +1500
- **Tint**: +2.0
- **Saturation**: 55
- **Contrast**: 1.15

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure to ensure detail in the iris is visible without blowing out highlights.
2. Shift the Temperature slider toward yellow/orange to achieve the warm-toned skin tones.
3. Use an HSL Qualifier to select the iris and slightly increase saturation and contrast to make the color pop.
4. Apply a soft-curve in the Curves tool to lift shadows while maintaining warmth.
5. Add a subtle circular vignette to draw the viewer eye directly to the pupil.
