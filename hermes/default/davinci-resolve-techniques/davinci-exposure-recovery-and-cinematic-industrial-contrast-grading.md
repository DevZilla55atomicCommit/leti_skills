# Exposure Recovery and Cinematic Industrial Contrast Grading

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C2sIMhgM0jl` (Gimbal_Moves)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Exposure Recovery and Cinematic Industrial Contrast Grading](C2sIMhgM0jl.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Wheels
- HDR Wheels
- Color Curves
- Power Windows

## Parameters

- **Highlights**: Decreased to recover detail
- **Contrast**: Increased
- **Saturation**: Slight increase
- **Color Balance**: Teal in shadows, Warm in highlights

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use the HDR wheels or Primary wheels to pull down highlights and recover detail in the light flare
2. Increase lift/offset to deepen the shadows for a moody look
3. Apply an S-curve in the Curves tool to increase contrast
4. Use Color Wheels to add a subtle teal tint to the shadows and a warm orange tint to the highlights
5. Add a vignette to draw focus to the subject in the center
