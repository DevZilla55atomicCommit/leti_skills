# High-Saturation Warm Vlog-Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `CiS08VyAKlH` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- HSL Curves
- Color Wheels
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **saturation**: +25
- **temperature**: +100
- **contrast**: +0.15
- **pivot**: 0.55

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure to balance the bright highlights from the sunroof.
2. Increase Temperature toward warm/orange for a sun-drenched feel.
3. Use HSL Curves to target the orange in the hoodie and increase its saturation without affecting skin tones.
4. Increase contrast and slightly lift the blacks to create a cinematic pop.
5. Apply a subtle soft vignette to focus attention on the subject face.
