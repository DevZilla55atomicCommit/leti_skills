# Macro Contrast & Teal-Organic Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C-AhaZuxW1j` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Macro Contrast & Teal-Organic Grade](C-AhaZuxW1j.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves Contrast
- Qualifier Mask
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Saturation**: 1.2
- **Contrast**: 1.25
- **Midtone Tint**: Teal/Cyan
- **Skin Tone Warmth**: Warm/Orange

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a primary grade to balance exposure and neutralize skin tones.
2. Use the Curves node to increase S-curve contrast, deepening the blacks of the pupil.
3. Use a Qualifier to select only the iris and shift the hue toward teal and green.
4. Apply a circular Power Window on the iris to slightly increase saturation and sharpen the texture detail.
5. Add a slight warm tint to the highlights of the skin areas to contrast with the cool eye.
