# Cinematic Teal & Orange Urban Glow

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C6HiHIHvTfh` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Teal & Orange Urban Glow](C6HiHIHvTfh.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels (Teal/Orange)
- Halation/Glow
- Shar

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Pivot**: 0.550
- **Saturation**: 450
- **Midtone Tint**: 0.020
- **Glow Spread**: 0.50
- **Glow Bias**: 0.15

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure and contrast to normalize the urban daylight footage.
2. Use Color Wheels to push shadows toward a subtle teal and highlights toward a warm orange/yellow.
3. Apply a slight desaturation to the overall image to achieve a film look.
4. Add a Glow node with a soft spread and high threshold to bloom the highlights on the buildings.
5. Apply a touch of sharpening in the Mid-tone tab to define architectural details.
