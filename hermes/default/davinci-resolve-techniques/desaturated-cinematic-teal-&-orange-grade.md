# Desaturated Cinematic Teal & Orange Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `DGgVaDWSTMg`
**Resolve Page:** Color
**Difficulty:** beginner
**Node Graph Type:** serial

## Overview
DaVinci Resolve technique extracted from Instagram Reel analysis.

## Key Nodes
- Primary Correction
- Color Wheels
- Hue Saturation

## Parameters
- **Saturation:** -20
- **Contrast:** +15
- **Shadows Tint:** Teal/Blue
- **Highlights Tint:** Warm/Orange

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Import the cityscape clip and move to the Color page
2. Use the Primary Wheels to lower the overall saturation for a desaturated look
3. Adjust the Lift wheel to push shadows toward teal/blue
4. Adjust the Gain wheel to push highlights toward a warm orange/yellow
5. Use the Hue vs Saturation curve to desaturate greens and yellows in the city
6. Apply a slight vignette to draw focus to the twin towers
