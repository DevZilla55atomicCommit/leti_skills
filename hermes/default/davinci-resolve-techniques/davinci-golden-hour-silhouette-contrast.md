# Golden Hour Silhouette Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C_FdS-YTNW6` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Golden Hour Silhouette Contrast](C_FdS-YTNW6.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Wheels
- Curves
- Color Warper
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.45
- **saturation**: 65.0
- **pivot**: 0.55
- **lift_tint**: Orange/Amber
- **gain_tint**: Yellow/Gold

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a Power Window circular mask around the subject to drop exposure, creating a silhouette.
2. Increase contrast and adjust the pivot to deepen shadows while keeping highlights bright.
3. Use the Color Wheels to push a warm amber/orange tint into the Gain and Highlights.
4. Use the Hue vs Sat curve to boost the saturation specifically in the yellows and oranges of the sky.
5. Add a slight Glow effect in the Fusion tab or via Open to simulate atmospheric diffusion around the sun.
