# Golden Hour Backlight Silhouette

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9bVX2CSYki` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Golden Hour Backlight Silhouette](C9bVX2CSYki.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- LUT

## Parameters

- **Exposure**: 1.2
- **LUT**: Warm Golden

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a warm golden LUT to the clip for golden hour tones
2. Increase exposure to simulate sun flare behind the subject
3. Use a silhouette mask to darken the subject while preserving hair highlights
