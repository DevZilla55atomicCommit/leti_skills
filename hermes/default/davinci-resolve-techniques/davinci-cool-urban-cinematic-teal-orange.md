# Cool Urban Cinematic Teal & Orange

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C6_8D6SNVuf` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Cool Urban Cinematic Teal & Orange](C6_8D6SNVuf.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- Curves
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **saturation**: 0.45
- **contrast**: 1.2
- **lift_tint**: Blue/Cyan
- **gain_tint**: Warm/Orange
- **temp**: -500

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure and balance using Primary Wheels to normalize the image.
2. Use the Lift wheel to push shadows toward a subtle cool teal/blue tone.
3. Use the Gain wheel to push highlights toward a subtle warm/orange tone to protect skin tones.
4. Apply a slight S-curve in the Curves tool to increase contrast.
5. Decrease global saturation slightly.
6. Add a soft vignette to draw focus to the subject in the center frame.
