# Wide-Angle POV Commercial Clean-Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C2TxCsEPGJD` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Wide-Angle POV Commercial Clean-Look](C2TxCsEPGJD.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- Primary Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **exposure**: +0.20
- **contrast**: 1.15
- **saturation**: 0.45
- **midtone_tint**: Cool Blue
- **highlight_tint**: Warm White

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure to recover detail in the bright polished floor reflections.
2. Use the Curves to slightly increase contrast while lifting the blacks for a clean look.
3. Apply a slight blue tint to the shadows using the Color Wheels to achieve a modern aesthetic.
4. Reduce overall saturation to create a desaturated, high-end commercial grade.
5. Use a Power Window on the floor area to slightly increase exposure and enhance the reflection of the overhead lights.
