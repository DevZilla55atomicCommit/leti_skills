# Golden Hour Silhouette & Warm Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C29SwD9hmJs` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Golden Hour Silhouette & Warm Contrast](C29SwD9hmJs.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Curves
- Power Windows
- Qualifier

## Parameters

- **Temperature**: 6500K+
- **Tint**: -2.0
- **Contrast**: 1.25
- **Saturation**: 1.1
- **Midtone Highlight**: Warm Orange

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust Temperature toward yellow/orange to enhance the sunset glow.
2. Use a circular Window on the subject to increase exposure slightly while maintaining the silhouette effect.
3. Apply an S-curve in the Curves tool to deepen shadows and pop highlights for cinematic contrast.
4. Use a teal-orange split by cooling the shadows slightly while keeping highlights warm.
5. Add a Glow effect or diffusion to the highlights to mimic the lens flare bloom.
