# Cinematic Teal & Orange High Contrast Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C5_EUg1L9WO` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Teal & Orange High Contrast Look](C5_EUg1L9WO.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Parallel Mixer
- Color Wheels
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.25
- **saturation**: 150
- **lift_color**: Teal/Blue
- **mid_color**: Warm/Orange
- **pivot**: 0.45

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a primary grade node to increase contrast and drop blacks for deep shadows.
2. Use the Lift wheel to push shadows toward a cold blue/cyan tint.
3. Use the Midtones wheel to push skin tones toward a warm orange/yellow tint.
4. Add a Parallel Mixer to isolate the subjects and increase their saturation specifically without affecting the cool shadows.
5. Apply a slight vignette using a Power Window to draw attention to the center.
