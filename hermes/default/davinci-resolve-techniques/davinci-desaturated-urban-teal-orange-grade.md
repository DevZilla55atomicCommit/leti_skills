# Desaturated Urban Teal & Orange Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `DCbhYRyIRKU` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Desaturated Urban Teal & Orange Grade](DCbhYRyIRKU.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Wheels
- Power Windows

## Parameters

- **Saturation**: -30
- **Temperature**: -5
- **Contrast**: 1.15

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure using primaries to balance shadows and highlights
2. Lower the overall saturation to achieve a muted look
3. Shift the shadows toward blue/teal using the Lift wheel
4. Use the Gain wheel to add a very slight warmth to highlights to keep skin tones natural
5. Apply a slight vignette to draw focus to the subject
