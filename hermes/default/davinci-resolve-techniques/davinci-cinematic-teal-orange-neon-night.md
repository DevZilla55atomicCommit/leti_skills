# Cinematic Teal & Orange Neon Night

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9HkWK5IPxc` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Teal & Orange Neon Night](C9HkWK5IPxc.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- Curves
- Hue vs. Saturation
- Glow

## Parameters

- **lift**: -0.02
- **gamma**: 0.05
- **gain**: 0.1
- **saturation**: 0.65
- **contrast**: 1.2
- **shadow_tint**: Blue/Cyan
- **highlight_tint**: Orange/Red

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply an S-curve in the Curves tool to increase contrast.
2. Use the Color Wheels to push Shadows toward a deep teal and Highlights toward a vibrant orange/red.
3. Lift the blacks slightly to fade the shadows for a cinematic matte look.
4. Use Hue vs. Saturation to boost the saturation of the red neon signs while keeping skin tones natural.
5. Add a Glow node with a soft threshold and low opacity to simulate the neon light bleed.
6. Add a vignette to draw focus to the subject in the center of the frame.
