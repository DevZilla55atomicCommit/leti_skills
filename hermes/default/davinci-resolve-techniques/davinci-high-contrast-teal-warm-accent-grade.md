# High-Contrast Teal & Warm Accent Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C89ISegyhkd` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![High-Contrast Teal & Warm Accent Grade](C89ISegyhkd.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Color Qualifier

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.25
- **saturation**: 0.65
- **shadows_tint**: Teal
- **highlights_tint**: Warm/Orange
- **midtone_lift**: Yellow-ish
- **log_curve_midpoint**: 0.45

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight S-curve in the Curves tool to deepen water shadows.
2. Use the Lift wheel to add a subtle blue/teal tint to the shadows.
3. Use the Gain wheel to add a warm yellow/orange tint to the highlights.
4. Use a Color Qualifier to isolate the yellow shirt and increase its saturation specifically to make the subject pop.
5. Reduce overall saturation to give the water and wood-tones a moody, desaturated look.
