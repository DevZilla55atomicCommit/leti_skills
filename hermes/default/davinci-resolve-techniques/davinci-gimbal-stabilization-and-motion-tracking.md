# Gimbal Stabilization and Motion Tracking

**Source:** Instagram Reel `Cv_zKvEtHAy` (Gimbal_Moves)  
**Page:** Edit | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Gimbal Stabilization and Motion Tracking](Cv_zKvEtHAy.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Camera Motion
- Stabilizer

## Parameters

- **stabilization_mode**: Perspective
- **smoothness**: 50
- **zoom**: 1.1

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Import the gimbal footage into the Timeline
2. Select the clip and open the Inspector
3. Navigate to the Stabilizer tab
4. Click 'Stabilize' to remove camera shake
5. Adjust Smoothness and Zoom to hide cropped edges
