# Warm Skin Tones with Teal Recessional Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9WrvFEMz4v` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Warm Skin Tones with Teal Recessional Contrast](C9WrvFEMz4v.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Wheels
- Hue Saturation Curves
- Curves
- Qualifier

## Parameters

- **lift_offset**: -0.02
- **midtone_tint**: +0.05
- **skin_tone_saturation**: 1.2
- **teal_orange_shift**: complementary split

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight lift to the blacks to create a faded, cinematic shadow look.
2. Use the Color Wheels to push shadows and greens toward a teal/cyan hue.
3. Use the Hue Saturation curves to isolate skin tones and increase their saturation/warmth specifically.
4. Use a Qualifier or Power Window to de-saturate the background slightly while keeping the subject skin tones vibrant.
5. Add a subtle vignette to draw focus to the subject face.
