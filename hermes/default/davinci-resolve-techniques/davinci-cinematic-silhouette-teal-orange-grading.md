# Cinematic Silhouette & Teal/Orange Grading

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C6Jctt_xni6` (Footage_Collection)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Silhouette & Teal/Orange Grading](C6Jctt_xni6.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Qualifier
- Power Window
- Color Wheels

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: High
- **Saturation**: Boosted
- **Shadows**: Teal/Blue
- **Highlights**: Orange/Pink

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust the primary wheels to increase contrast and drop the black point to silhouette the foreground plant.
2. Use the Lift wheel to push the shadows toward a deep teal/blue hue.
3. Use the Gain wheel to push the highlights toward a warm orange/pink.
4. Apply a Power Window (circular or free) around the plant to slightly increase its exposure or sharpness to define the silhouette.
5. Use the Blur tool on a separate node for the background layers to simulate a shallow depth of field.
6. Add a slight vignette to draw focus to the center-weighted subject.
