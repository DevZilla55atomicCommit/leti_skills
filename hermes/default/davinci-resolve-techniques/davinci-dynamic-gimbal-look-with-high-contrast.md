# Dynamic Gimbal Look with High Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8-Az1DgFI3` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Dynamic Gimbal Look with High Contrast](C8-Az1DgFI3.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- HSL Wheels
- Color Curves
- Power Windows

## Parameters

- **Saturation**: 150
- **Contrast**: 1.20
- **Gamma**: Pushed up slightly
- **Red Saturation**: Increased

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a primary grade to balance exposure and white balance
2. Increase contrast using the Contrast slider to make blacks pop
3. Use the HSL Qualifier to target and boost the red and maroon tones specifically
4. Apply a soft Power Window on the performer to slightly increase exposure and sharpness on them
5. Add a subtle vignette to draw focus to the center action
