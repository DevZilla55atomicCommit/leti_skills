## Parameters

- **movement_speed**: slow-linear
- **pan_rotation**: counter-directional
- **stabilization**: perspective-camera

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Mount camera on a motorized gimbal stabilizer
2. Position the subject relative to a fixed focal point or architectural feature
3. Walk slowly in a straight line or arc (the drift)
4. Rotate the gimbal/camera in the opposite direction to keep the subject framed
5. Use Stabilization tool in DaVinci Resolve to smooth out any remaining micro-jitters
