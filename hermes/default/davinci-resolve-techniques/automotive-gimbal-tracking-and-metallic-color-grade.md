# Automotive Gimbal Tracking & Metallic Color Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `DG5M8Mhocoz`  
**Resolve Page:** Color  
**Difficulty:** intermediate  
**Node Graph Type:** serial

## Key Nodes
- Primary Correction
- Power Window (Car)
- Qualifier
- Color Wheels

## Parameters
- **Saturation:** Increased
- **Contrast:** High
- **Midtone Detail:** Increased for paint texture
- **Highlights:** Softened to manage reflections

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Import gimbal-stabilized footage into the Color page
2. Apply a Power Window to the car to isolate the body
3. Use a Qualifier to select the specific gold/bronze paint tones
4. Increase saturation and midtone detail to make metallic flakes pop
5. Apply a slight vignette to draw focus to the vehicle lines
