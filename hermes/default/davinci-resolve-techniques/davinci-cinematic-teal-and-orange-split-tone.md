# Cinematic Teal and Orange Split Tone

**Source:** Instagram Reel `DCaPTNgv04S` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Teal and Orange Split Tone](DCaPTNgv04S.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- Power Mask
- Qualifier

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: High
- **Saturation**: Enhanced
- **Shadows**: Deep Teal
- **Highlights**: Warm Orange
- **Midtones**: Peach

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Import clip and perform a primary balance to fix exposure and white balance
2. Use Color Wheels to push shadows toward Teal and highlights/midtones toward Orange
3. Add a Pastel Curves node to lift the blacks slightly for a faded cinematic look
4. Apply a Power Mask or Qualifier to the vehicle headlights to increase exposure and glow specifically
5. Use the Glow effect on the bright highlights to simulate atmospheric light bleed
6. Increase saturation globally to enhance the vibrant tones of the sky
