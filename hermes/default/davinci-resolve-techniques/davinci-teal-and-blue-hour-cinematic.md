# Teal and Blue Hour Cinematic

**Source:** Instagram Reel `Cgc6vevgr2M` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Teal and Blue Hour Cinematic](Cgc6vevgr2M.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels (HSL)
- Curves
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **shadow_tint**: Teal/Blue
- **midtone_tint**: Neutral/Warm
- **saturation**: 1.2
- **contrast**: 1.15
- **pivot**: 0.5

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight S-curve in the Curves tool to establish contrast.
2. Use the Lift wheel to push shadows toward a deep teal/blue hue.
3. Increase saturation specifically in the Blue HSL curves to pop the Santorini dome and sea.
4. Use a Qualifier to protect the white buildings, ensuring they remain clean but not overly blown out.
5. Add a soft circular vignette using the Power Window tool to draw focus to the subject in the center.
