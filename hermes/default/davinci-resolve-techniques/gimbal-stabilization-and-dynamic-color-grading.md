# Gimbal Stabilization and Dynamic Color Grading

**Source:** Instagram Reel `DHLSAxAIQ9O`

## Parameters
- **stabilization:** Perspective/Similarity
- **contrast:** High
- **saturation:** Slight boost

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Mount camera on a gimbal for smooth low-angle tracking shots
2. Import footage into DaVinci Resolve Color page
3. Use the Stabilizer tool in the Inspector tab to smooth out any remaining micro-jitters
4. Apply primary color correction to balance blacks and highlights on the car carbon fiber texture
5. Add a LUT or manual grade to achieve a cinematic automotive look
