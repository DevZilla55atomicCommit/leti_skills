# High-Contrast Athletic Pop with Selective Saturation

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C7TzP3ZNArp` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![High-Contrast Athletic Pop with Selective Saturation](C7TzP3ZNArp.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary
- Curves
- Color Warper
- Qualifier

## Parameters

- **Saturation**: 150
- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Midtone Detail**: +20
- **Highlights-Tint**: Warm/Yellow

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a Lens Blur or Power Window to the background to simulate shallow depth of field.
2. Use the Curves tool to increase contrast and deepen the blacks for a punchy look.
3. Use the Qualifier (HSL) to isolate the pink/magenta tones and increase saturation and luminance specifically.
4. Adjust the Color Warper to add a slight warm tint to the highlights while keeping shadows cool.
5. Increase Midtone Detail to enhance the texture of the athletic gear and skin.
