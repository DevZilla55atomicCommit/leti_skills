# Soft Overcast Urban Naturalist Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C14zVmHRVvA` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Soft Overcast Urban Naturalist Look](C14zVmHRVvA.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Balance
- Color Wheels
- Power Windows

## Parameters

- **lift**: -0.02
- **gamma**: 0.05
- **gain**: -0.03
- **saturation**: 430
- **temp**: 5500

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight lift to the blacks to soften the contrast of the overcast sky.
2. Use a slight blue tint in the shadows (lift) to enhance the cool tones of the environment.
3. Decrease overall saturation slightly to achieve a muted, professional urban aesthetic.
4. Use a Power Window on the subject to slightly increase exposure and warmth, making her pop from the flat background.
5. Adjust color temperature toward neutral/warm to keep skin tones natural against the cool background.
