# Soft Natural Skin Tone & Clean Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8Koi8Uup6v` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Soft Natural Skin Tone & Clean Contrast](C8Koi8Uup6v.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Skin Qualifier
- Contrast Curves
- Soft Glow

## Parameters

- **exposure**: +0.15
- **saturation**: 1.15
- **midtone_lift**: warm/orange
- **glow_radius**: 0.50

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure and balance using primary wheels to achieve a clean white point.
2. Use the Skin Tone Hue qualifier to ensure facial tones are natural and not oversaturated.
3. Apply a slight S-curve in the Curves tool to deepen blacks and pop highlights.
4. Add a Glow node with a low threshold and high softness to soften the highlights on the skin.
5. Slightly warm the midtones to give a healthy lifestyle feel.
