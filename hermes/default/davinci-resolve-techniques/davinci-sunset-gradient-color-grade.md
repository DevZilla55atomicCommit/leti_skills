# Sunset Gradient Color Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `Cy-autrovsg` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

- Color Balance

## Parameters

- **gradient_direction**: horizontal

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply horizontal gradient from top to bottom with warm tones (orange/yellow)
2. Adjust color balance to enhance orange/yellow hues and reduce cool tones
