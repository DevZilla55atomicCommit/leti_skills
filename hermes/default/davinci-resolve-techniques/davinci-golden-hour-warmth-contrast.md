# Golden Hour Warmth & Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C6oiPrUO7MQ` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Golden Hour Warmth & Contrast](C6oiPrUO7MQ.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Qualifiers (Hue/Saturation/Luma)

## Parameters

- **temperature**: +1500
- **saturation**: +15
- **contrast**: 1.2
- **midtone_pivot**: 0.55
- **shadows_tint**: Teal slight

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust Temperature towards the warm to enhance the sunset glow.
2. Use the Lift wheel to crush shadows slightly while adding a subtle blue tint.
3. Apply a Qualifier to select the red jacket and increase its saturation and luminance to make it pop.
4. Use an S-curve in the Curves tool to increase contrast specifically in the desert highlights.
5. Add a slight vignette to draw focus toward the performing child.
