# Muted Cinematic Warm with Soft Highlights

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C_JB6UrPutM` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Muted Cinematic Warm with Soft Highlights](C_JB6UrPutM.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Exposure
- Color Balance
- Curves Contrast
- Hue Saturation Luminance
- Vignette/De-haze

## Parameters

- **lift**: +0.015
- **gamma**: -0.010
- **gain**: +0.020
- **saturation**: 45
- **midtone_tint**: Warm Orange

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust Lift/Gamma to slightly lift the shadows for a faded film look.
2. Use the Curves tool to create a subtle S-curve for contrast without losing highlight detail.
3. Use Hue Saturation Luminance to desaturate the greens and yellows in the background.
4. Apply a warm tint to the midtones/skin tones to enhance the golden-hour feel.
5. Add a subtle vignette to focus attention on the center.
