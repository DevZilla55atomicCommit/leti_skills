# Golden Hour Silhouette Rim Light

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C86_0RkxdVY` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Golden Hour Silhouette Rim Light](C86_0RkxdVY.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- Primary Balance
- Power Window Rim
- Color Wheels
- Halation

## Parameters

- **Exposure**: -1.5
- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Pivot**: 0.45
- **Highlights**: Orange/Gold
- **Shadows**: Teal/Blue
- **Halation_Amount**: 0.4

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Lower overall exposure to underexpose the subject into a silhouette.
2. Use a Power Window (circular mask) around the hair and edges to isolate the rim light.
3. Increase highlights and saturation specifically within that window to pop the golden glow.
4. Apply a warm color tint: push oranges into highlights and slight teals into shadows.
5. Lift the blacks slightly in the Curves tool to create a faded, film-like look.
6. Add a subtle Halation or Glow effect to simulate light bleed around the bright rim edges.
