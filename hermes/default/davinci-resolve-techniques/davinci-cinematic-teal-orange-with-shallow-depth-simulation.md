# Cinematic Teal & Orange with Shallow Depth Simulation

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C_rRo6rtrTE` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Teal & Orange with Shallow Depth Simulation](C_rRo6rtrTE.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- Power Windows
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **lift_offset**: Blue/Teal
- **gain_offset**: Warm/Orange
- **midtone_**: Neutral
- **saturation**: 1.15
- **contrast**: 1.2

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight S-curve in the Curves tool to increase contrast.
2. Use the Color Wheels to push shadows (Lift) toward a teal/blue hue.
3. Use the Gain wheel to push highlights toward a warm orange/gold hue.
4. Apply a Power Window on the subject to slightly increase exposure and sharpness locally.
5. Add a soft vignette to draw focus to the center of the camera frame.
6. Increase saturation specifically in the blue and orange channels to make the water and skin tones pop.
