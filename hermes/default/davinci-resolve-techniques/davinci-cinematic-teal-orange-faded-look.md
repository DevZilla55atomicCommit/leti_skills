# Cinematic Teal & Orange Faded Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9918AeRzBy` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Teal & Orange Faded Look](C9918AeRzBy.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Curves (Contrast)
- Color Wheels (Split)
- Parade

## Parameters

- **Lift**: Teal/Cyan tint
- **Gamma**: Warm/Orange tint
- **Gain**: Golden/Yellow
- **Saturation**: 0.45
- **Contrast**: 1.15

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Lift the black point slightly in the Primaries wheels to create a faded matte black look.
2. Use the Curves tool to create a subtle S-curve for natural contrast.
3. In the Color Wheels, push the Lift toward teal/cyan to add cool tones to shadows.
4. Push the Gamma and Gain wheels toward orange/yellow to warm up midtones and highlights.
5. Use a Hue Qualifier to isolate skin tones and ensure they remain warm while the environment stays cool.
6. Apply a slight vignette to draw focus to the subject.
