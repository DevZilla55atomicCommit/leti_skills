# Moody High-Contrast Teal & Orange Interior

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8owaSSOQ9q` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Moody High-Contrast Teal & Orange Interior](C8owaSSOQ9q.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- Contrast
- Color Wheels
- Qualifiers
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **lift**: lifted slightly for faded blacks
- **contrast**: high ratio
- **saturation**: desaturated globally
- **shadow_tint**: Teal/Blue
- **highlight_tint**: Warm/Yellow

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use the Primaries wheels to slightly lift the blacks, creating a matte film look.
2. Increase contrast to separate the window light from the dark interior seats.
3. Apply a Color Wheels node to push shadows toward a teal/cyan hue and highlights toward a warm tone.
4. Use a Qualifier to select only the window area and slightly increase the saturation to make the outdoor greenery pop.
5. Add a subtle Vignette to draw focus toward the subject and the window.
