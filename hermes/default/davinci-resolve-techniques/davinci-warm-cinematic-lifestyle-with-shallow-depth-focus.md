# Warm Cinematic Lifestyle with Shallow Depth Focus

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C-VZeGpIi3t` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Warm Cinematic Lifestyle with Shallow Depth Focus](C-VZeGpIi3t.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Wheels
- Hue Curves
- Color Mixer
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.2
- **saturation**: 1.1
- **midtone_tint**: Warm/Orange
- **shadow_lift**: Slight lift
- **vignette**: Soft-edged

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight blur to background elements in post-production to simulate shallow depth of field if not captured in-lens.
2. Use Primary Wheels to lift the blacks slightly for a matte cinematic look.
3. Increase warmth in the midtones and highlights to enhance skin tones and wood wood elements.
4. Use a Hue Saturation curve to slightly desaturate cool blues while keeping the oranges/browns vibrant.
5. Apply a soft vignette to draw focus to the central steam wand and mug.
