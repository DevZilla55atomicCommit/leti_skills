# Desert Cinematic High-Contrast Warmth

**Source:** Instagram Reel `Cvp42nhgOIh` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Desert Cinematic High-Contrast Warmth](Cvp42nhgOIh.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- HSL Qualifier
- Power / Vignette

## Parameters

- **Saturation**: 0.75
- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Lift**: Cool/Blue tint
- **Gain**: Warm/Orange tint
- **Midtones**: Saturated Earth

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight teal tint to the shadows to contrast with the warm desert sand.
2. Use the Curves tool to increase contrast, creating a slight S-curve for cinematic pop.
3. Use an HSL Qualifier to select the desert/sand areas and increase saturation and warmth specifically.
4. Add a subtle vignette to draw focus to the subject and the camera gear in the center-left.
5. Lower the highlights in the sky to prevent clipping of the clouds, maintaining a soft, airy look.
