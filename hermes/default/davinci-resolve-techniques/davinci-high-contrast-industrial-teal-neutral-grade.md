# High-Contrast Industrial Teal & Neutral Grade

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure Correction

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.25
- **saturation**: 0.8
- **lift**: -0.02
- **gamma**: 0.1
- **midtone_tint**: Cyan/Blue

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight lift-reduction to deepen the blacks of the camera body.
2. Use the Curves tool to create an S-curve for increased contrast in the highlights.
3. Desaturate the overall image slightly to emphasize the industrial feel.
4. Add a subtle cyan/blue tint to the shadows and midtones to mimic the digital display glow.
5. Apply a soft circular vignette to focus attention on the center where the lens settings are.
