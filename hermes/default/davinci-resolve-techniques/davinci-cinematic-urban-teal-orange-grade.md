# Cinematic Urban Teal & Orange Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C-LNbOwPyAo` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Urban Teal & Orange Grade](C-LNbOwPyAo.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Wheels
- Curves
- Color Mixer
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Saturation**: 0.8
- **Shadow_Tint**: Teal/Blue
- **Highlight_Tint**: Warm/Orange
- **Midtone_Hue**: Skin protection

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight contrast boost and lift the blacks using the Primary Wheels
2. Use the Curves node to create an S-curve for depth
3. In the Color Mixer, push shadows toward cyan and highlights toward orange/yellow
4. Apply a Power Window on the subject face to slightly increase exposure and warm skin tones
5. Reduce overall global saturation to achieve a muted cinematic look
