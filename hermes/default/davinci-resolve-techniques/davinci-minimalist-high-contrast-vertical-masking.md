# Minimalist High-Contrast Vertical Masking

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8pJ33RROJu` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Minimalist High-Contrast Vertical Masking](C8pJ33RROJu.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Mask

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 2.0
- **saturation**: 1.5
- **lift_color**: Deep Black
- **mask_softness**: 0.05

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a Power Mask or Window to a narrow vertical rectangle in the center of the frame.
2. Increase the softness of the mask edge slightly to prevent harsh digital against the black.
3. Use the Curves tool to push all non-masked areas to pure black.
4. Use the Midtone wheel to add a warm orange/gold tint to the masked area only.
5. Apply a slight vertical gradient within the masked area to simulate a sunset sky.
