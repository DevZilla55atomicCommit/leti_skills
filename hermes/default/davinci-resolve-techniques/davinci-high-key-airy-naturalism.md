# High-Key Airy Naturalism

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9NjLOesWB3` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![High-Key Airy Naturalism](C9NjLOesWB3.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure Adjustment
- White Balance
- Curves Contrast
- Color Wheels Pastel
- Soft Glow

## Parameters

- **exposure**: +0.40
- **contrast**: -0.15
- **saturation**: 0.85
- **lift_offset**: +0.02
- **tint**: Warm (Orange/Yellow)

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Increase exposure slightly to achieve a high-key, bright look.
2. Use the Lift tool in the Color Wheels to slightly lift the shadows, preventing true blacks.
3. Lower the overall contrast to soften the transition between highlights and shadows.
4. Desaturate the image slightly, keeping the skin tones and white flower petals natural.
5. Apply a Glow node with a high threshold and low opacity to create a soft, ethereal bloom around the window highlights.
