# Teal and Orange Variant with Neon Pop

**Source:** Instagram Reel `CmAaQsJAkKN` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Teal and Orange Variant with Neon Pop](CmAaQsJAkKN.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Qualifiers
- Color Wheels
- Power Windows

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: 1.20
- **Pivot**: 0.45
- **Shadows**: Cool/Blue tint
- **Highlights**: Warm/Neutral tint
- **Saturation**: 0.85

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust Contrast and Pivot to deepen the blacks of the subject black clothing.
2. Use the Lift/Gain wheels to push shadows toward a subtle blue and highlights toward a slight warm tone.
3. Use a Qualifier to select only the red elements in the poster and increase their saturation and luminance specifically.
4. Apply a Power Window around the subject to slightly lift skin tones to ensure they pop against the moody background.
5. Add a slight vignette to draw focus toward the center of the frame.
