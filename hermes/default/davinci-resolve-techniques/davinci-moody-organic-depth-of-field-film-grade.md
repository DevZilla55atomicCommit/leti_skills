# Moody Organic Depth of Field & Film Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C-3Rg1TsYRj` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Moody Organic Depth of Field & Film Grade](C-3Rg1TsYRj.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Qualifier Mask
- Soft Blur

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.20
- **saturation**: 0.75
- **lift_tint**: Teal/Green
- **gain_tint**: Warm Orange
- **soft_blur_radius**: 15.0

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight S-curve in Curves to increase contrast while lifting the blacks.
2. Use the Color Wheels to tint the shadows toward a deep green for a cinematic feel.
3. Use a Qualifier to isolate skin tones and slightly increase warmth/saturation there while keeping the background cool.
4. Add a Soft Blur effect applied to the background layer to simulate a shallow depth of field if not captured in-camera.
5. Add a subtle film grain overlay to enhance the organic film-like texture.
