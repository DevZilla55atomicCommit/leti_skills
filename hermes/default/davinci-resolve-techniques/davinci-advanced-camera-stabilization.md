# Advanced Camera Stabilization

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C3eicXQvD1X` (Gimbal_Moves)  
**Page:** Edit | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Advanced Camera Stabilization](C3eicXQvD1X.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Inspector

## Parameters

- **Stabilization**: On
- **Camera Motion**: Perspective
- **Smoothing**: 50.0

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Import the shaky clip into the Edit timeline
2. Open the Inspector panel
3. Navigate to the Stabilization tab
4. Click the Stabilization button
5. Set Camera Motion to 'Perspective' or 'Similarity' for the best results
6. Adjust the Smoothing value to remove jitter without excessive cropping
7. Click 'Zoom' to hide black edges caused by stabilization
