# High-Contrast Golden Hour Silhouette

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C89eMwOMthE` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![High-Contrast Golden Hour Silhouette](C89eMwOMthE.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Soft Glow

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: High
- **Shadows**: Crushed Black
- **Saturation**: Reduced
- **Temperature**: Warm

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use the Primary wheels to pull the Lifts and Shadows down until the silhouette is pure black
2. Increase Contrast to sharpen the edge between the silhouette and the sky
3. Use a Qualifier or simply use the Color Wheels to add a warm orange/yellow tint to the Highlights and a subtle teal/blue tint to the Shadows
4. Apply a Glow effect on a separate node to diffuse the atmospheric light in the sky
5. Use a vignette to further draw focus to the center of the silhouette edge
