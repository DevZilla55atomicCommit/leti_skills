# High-Contrast Natural Teal & Airy Travel

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C7uLEIbK7q5` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![High-Contrast Natural Teal & Airy Travel](C7uLEIbK7q5.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Saturation**: 1.1
- **Lift**: Slight blue tint
- **Highlights**: Warm/Yellow tint
- **Midtones**: Natural skin tone preservation

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure using Primaries to ensure the white plane is bright without clipping highlights.
2. Use the Curves tool to create a slight S-curve for deep blacks and whites.
3. Apply a subtle blue tint to the Shadows (Lift) and a warm tint to the Highlights for a cinematic contrast.
4. Use a Power Window (circle) around the subject to slightly increase exposure and make her pop from the background.
5. Increase saturation specifically in the blue channel to make the sky appear more vibrant.
