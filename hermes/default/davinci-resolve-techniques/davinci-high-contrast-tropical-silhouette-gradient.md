# High-Contrast Tropical Silhouette Gradient

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8CWdA2StKm` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![High-Contrast Tropical Silhouette Gradient](C8CWdA2StKm.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Lift**: -25.0
- **Gamma**: +10.0
- **Gain**: -15.0
- **Saturation**: 45.0
- **Contrast**: 65.0

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use the Lift tool to drop the shadows until the palm fronds are solid black silhouettes.
2. Apply a Linear Gradient mask to create a warm glow at the horizon and a deep tone at the top.
3. Use the Color Wheels to push the midtones toward a warm orange and the highlights toward a muted teal/grey.
4. Add an S-curve in the Curves tab to increase contrast and make the sky transition pop.
5. Lower the overall saturation to maintain a moody cinematic feel.
