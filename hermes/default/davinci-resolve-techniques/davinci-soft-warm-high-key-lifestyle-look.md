# Soft Warm-High-Key Lifestyle Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C1u4l1xPAyy` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Soft Warm-High-Key Lifestyle Look](C1u4l1xPAyy.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Wheels
- Curves
- Power Window
- Color Blur

## Parameters

- **Lift**: Slight lifted blacks
- **Gain**: Warm yellow/orange tint
- **Saturation**: 1.15
- **Softness**: Low diffusion on highlights

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a soft circular power window around the face to increase exposure and exposure slightly.
2. Use the Offset wheel to add a subtle warm tint to the entire image.
3. Adjust the Lift node to slightly lift the blacks for a faded cinematic feel.
4. Increase saturation specifically in the midtones to make skin tones and background colors pop.
5. Add a slight Blur node at the end of the chain with a low radius to soften skin textures without losing detail.
