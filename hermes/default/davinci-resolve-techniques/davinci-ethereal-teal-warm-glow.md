# Ethereal Teal & Warm Glow

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9hqHbpM2aH` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Ethereal Teal & Warm Glow](C9hqHbpM2aH.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Balance (Teal/Warm Split)
- Glow/Halation
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **saturation**: 0.8
- **contrast**: 1.2
- **soft_glow_threshold**: 0.45
- **shadow_tint**: Teal/Cyan
- **highlight_tint**: Warm Orange/Gold

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure and contrast to lift shadows slightly while maintaining skin detail.
2. Use the Color Wheels to push shadows toward cyan and highlights toward a warm orange.
3. Apply a Glow node with a soft spread and high threshold to create the ethereal highlight bleed.
4. Use the Blur tool or a Soft Focus effect on a separate layer to soften the overall mid-tones.
5. Add a subtle vignette to focus attention on the subject eyes.
