# Cinematic Teal and Orange Grading with Selective Focus

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C7MeYMXx3i2` (Footage_Collection)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Teal and Orange Grading with Selective Focus](C7MeYMXx3i2.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Wheels
- Qualifier
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: High
- **Saturation**: Boosted in reds
- **Shadows**: Teal tint
- **Highlights**: Orange/Gold tint

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Import clip and use a Primary Correction node to balance exposure and contrast.
2. Apply a Teal and Orange grade by pushing teals into the shadows and oranges into the highlights wheels.
3. Use a Qualifier to select the red flower and increase its saturation and luminance independently.
4. Apply a circular Power Window around the flower to slightly brighten it and make it pop.
5. Add a Blur effect to the background nodes to enhance the shallow depth of field shown.
