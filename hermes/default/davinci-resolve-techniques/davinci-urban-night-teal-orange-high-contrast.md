# Urban Night Teal & Orange High Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C2kvhTmvoyW` (Cinematic)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Urban Night Teal & Orange High Contrast](C2kvhTmvoyW.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balances
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.4
- **pivot**: 0.5
- **shadows_tint**: Teal/Blue
- **highlights_tint**: Warm/Yellow
- **saturation**: 45.0

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Increase global contrast and lower the blacks in Primaries to deepen the night sky.
2. Use the Curves node to lift the black point slightly for a matte black look.
3. Apply a Color Wheels adjustment: push shadows toward a deep blue and highlights toward a warm yellow/orange.
4. Use a Power Window around the subject to slightly drop exposure and increase blue tint to create the silhouette.
5. Add a slight glow or bloom effect in Fusion to soften the bright lights from the building windows.
