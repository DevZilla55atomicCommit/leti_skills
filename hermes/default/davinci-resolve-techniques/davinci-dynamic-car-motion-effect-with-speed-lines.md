# Dynamic Car Motion Effect with Speed Lines

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C5GPhahtuJj` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Fusion | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Dynamic Car Motion Effect with Speed Lines](C5GPhahtuJj.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Motion Blur
- Stroke

## Parameters

- **Motion Blur Amount**: 10%
- **Stroke Width**: 2

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply Motion Blur node to the car footage layer
2. Use Stroke node to generate speed lines on the road surface
