# Cinematic Teal and Orange Color Grading with Texture

**Source:** Instagram Reel `DBl18R1imiw` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Teal and Orange Color Grading with Texture](DBl18R1imiw.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Curves
- Color Wheels
- Qualifier

## Parameters

- **Shadows**: Shifted toward Teal/Blue
- **Highlights**: Shifted toward Warm/Orange
- **Saturation**: Reduced midtones
- **Contrast**: Increased for cinematic pop

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a primary node to balance exposure and white balance
2. Use the Color Wheels to push shadows into teal and highlights into orange/yellow
3. Use the Hue/Saturation curves to desaturate specific greens or blues in the background
4. Apply a qualifier to isolate skin tones to maintain warmth against the cool overall grade
5. Add a slight vignette to draw focus to the center
6. Add a fine film grain node for a cinematic texture look
