# Golden Hour Silhouette High Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9r40kwyncw` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Golden Hour Silhouette High Contrast](C9r40kwyncw.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Windows

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: High
- **Saturation**: Medium-High
- **Lift**: Warm/Orange
- **Shadows**: Crushed Black

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use the Lift wheel to pull the shadows down, ensuring buildings are completely silhouetted.
2. Use the Curves tool to create a strong S-curve, increasing contrast between sky and buildings.
3. Shift the Midtones and Gain wheels toward Orange and Yellow to establish the golden hour palette.
4. Apply a Power Window to the sky area to increase luminance and saturation to create the glow.
5. Add a slight Gaussian Blur to the foreground element to mimic the shallow depth of field seen in the shot.
