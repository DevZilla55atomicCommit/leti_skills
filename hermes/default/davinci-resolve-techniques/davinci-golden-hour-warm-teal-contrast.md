# Golden Hour Warm Teal Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C7_U_kksA0j` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Golden Hour Warm Teal Contrast](C7_U_kksA0j.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- HSL Qualifier
- Glow

## Parameters

- **Temperature**: +5 (Warm)
- **Saturation**: +1.2
- **Lift**: Slightly lifted for faded blacks
- **Highlights**: Warm/Orange tint
- **Shadows**: Teal tint

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use Primary Balance to warm the overall image and lift the blacks slightly.
2. Use Color Wheels to add teal to shadows and orange/yellow to the highlights.
3. Apply an HSL Qualifier to isolate the hair and increase saturation and luminance for red tones.
4. Add a Glow node with a soft threshold and high diffusion to create the dreamy light bleed.
5. Use a subtle vignette to draw focus to the center subject.
