# High-Contrast Warm Silhouette

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C-FhLNySZmG` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![High-Contrast Warm Silhouette](C-FhLNySZmG.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary_Wheels
- Curves
- Color Mixer
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: High
- **Saturation**: Low-Medium
- **Lift**: Deepened Blacks
- **Gain**: Warm/Amber
- **Temperature**: Increased (Warm)

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use the Lift slider to crush the shadows, ensuring the silhouette is deep black.
2. Increase contrast significantly in the Curves tool to separate the silhouette from the bright background.
3. Apply a warm tint to the Gain and Midtones using the Color Wheels (Orange/Yellow).
4. Use Power Window on the subject to further reduce exposure, ensuring they remain a silhouette.
5. Add a slight glow or bloom effect to the highlights to simulate the light bleeding through the blinds.
