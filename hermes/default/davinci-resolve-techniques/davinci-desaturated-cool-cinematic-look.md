# Desaturated Cool-Cinematic Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C4YBBbFIALf` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Desaturated Cool-Cinematic Look](C4YBBbFIALf.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Windows

## Parameters

- **Lift**: +0.020
- **Gamma Offset**: -0.05
- **Saturation**: 0.35
- **Temperature**: -5.0
- **Tint**: 2.0

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a Lift adjustment to slightly raise the blacks for a faded matte look.
2. Decrease overall saturation using the Saturation slider or Color Curves.
3. Shift the Temperature toward blue/cyan to achieve the cool atmosphere.
4. Use Hue/Saturation curves to specifically desaturate reds and yellows while keeping skin tones.
5. Apply a Power Window on the subject to slightly increase exposure and sharpness to pop the her.
6. Add a slight Glow effect in the Fusion tab to mimic a soft-diffusion filter.
