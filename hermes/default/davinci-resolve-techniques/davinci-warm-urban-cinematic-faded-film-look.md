# Warm Urban Cinematic Faded Film Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9thGRiNtzG` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Warm Urban Cinematic Faded Film Look](C9thGRiNtzG.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Wheels
- Curves
- Color Mixer
- Power Window Qualifier

## Parameters

- **Lift**: -0.02
- **Gamma**: 0.05
- **Gain**: 0.95
- **Saturation**: 0.45
- **Contrast**: 1.1

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Lift the black point in the RGB Curves tool slightly to create faded blacks.
2. Apply a warm orange tint to the highlights and a slight blue/teal tint to the shadows.
3. Use a Power Window on the subject to slightly increase exposure and sharpness to separate from the background.
4. Add a subtle vignette to draw focus to the center-de-focused subject.
5. Add a small amount of film grain in the effects tab to simulate analog film stock.
