# Muted Earthy Cinematic Film Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `CshGDUdgJKM` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Muted Earthy Cinematic Film Look](CshGDUdgJKM.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- Hue Curves
- Soft Glow

## Parameters

- **Lift**: 0.02 offset
- **Gamma**: -0.05
- **Saturation**: 0.75
- **Contrast**: 1.1
- **Temperature**: +500K

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Raise the black point point slightly to fade the shadows for a matte effect.
2. Decrease overall saturation to achieve a muted, high-fashion desert vibe.
3. Add a warm orange/yellow tint to the Highlights and a slight teal/blue tint to the Shadows for color separation.
4. Use Hue Curves to desaturate the greens and yellows while keeping skin tones warm and natural.
5. Apply a soft Glow or Halation effect to the highlights to diffuse the bright sky area.
