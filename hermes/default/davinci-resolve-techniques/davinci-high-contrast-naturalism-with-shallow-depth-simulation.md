# High-Contrast Naturalism with Shallow Depth Simulation

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Power Windows
- Color Mixer

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Saturation**: 0.85
- **ShadowLift**: Cool blue tint
- **Highlight**: Warm tint

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use a primary exposure node to balance the bright sunlight and protect the white dress from blowing out.
2. Apply a subtle curve to slightly deepen the S-curve for increased contrast against the stone texture.
3. Use a Power Window on the subject to slightly increase exposure and saturation to pop her from the neutral background.
4. Add a slight teal/cyan tint to the shadows using the Color Mixer to create a cinematic color-tone separation.
5. Desaturate the overall image slightly to maintain a sophisticated, high-fashion aesthetic.
