## Parameters

- **Speed Change**: Reverse

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Film yourself closing the door normally
2. Import the clip into the timeline
3. Right-click on the clip in the timeline
4. Select 'Speed Change'
5. Check the 'Reverse' checkbox
