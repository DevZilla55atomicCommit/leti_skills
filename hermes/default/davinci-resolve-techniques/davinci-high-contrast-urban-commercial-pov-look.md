# High-Contrast Urban Commercial POV Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9uqiC6NJxU` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![High-Contrast Urban Commercial POV Look](C9uqiC6NJxU.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Curves
- Hue Sat
- Color Wheels

## Parameters

- **saturation**: 0.65
- **contrast**: 1.2
- **midtone_pivot**: 0.5
- **orange_tint**: +0.1
- **green_saturation**: +0.25

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Balance exposure using Primaries to neutralize flat daylight.
2. Use the Curves node to slightly lift the blacks and compress the highlights for a cinematic range.
3. Apply Hue vs Saturation to specifically target and boost the orange of the parking lines and the green car.
4. Increase global contrast to make the urban elements pop against the grey asphalt.
5. Add a subtle vignette to draw focus inward to the center where the gimbal and car are located.
