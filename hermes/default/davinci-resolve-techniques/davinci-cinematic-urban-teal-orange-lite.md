# Cinematic Urban Teal & Orange Lite

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C7BzYkwNbcb` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Urban Teal & Orange Lite](C7BzYkwNbcb.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Color Wheels
- Power Windows
- Motion Blur

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Saturation**: 1.1
- **Shadow_Tint**: Teal/Blue
- **Highlight_Tint**: Warm/Orange
- **Midtone_Hue**: Skin tones

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a primary grade to balance exposure and contrast for bright daylight look
2. Use Color Wheels to push shadows toward a subtle blue and highlights toward a warm cream
3. Use a Power Window on the subjects to slightly increase exposure and sharpness to make them pop
4. Apply a Blur effect on a separate node for the background to enhance motion blur on the train
5. Increase saturation specifically in the blue channels using Hue vs Saturation
