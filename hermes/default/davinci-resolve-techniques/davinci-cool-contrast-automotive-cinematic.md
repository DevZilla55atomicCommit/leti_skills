# Cool-Contrast Automotive Cinematic

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8aq4fxvl6R` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Cool-Contrast Automotive Cinematic](C8aq4fxvl6R.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- Primary Balance
- Split Tone
- Color Wheels
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **contrast**: +0.15
- **saturation**: 45
- **shadow_tint**: Teal/Blue
- **highlight_tint**: Warm/Neutral
- **midtone_lift**: Slight lift

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure to ensure the white car is detailed without clipping highlights.
2. Use the Color Wheels to add a subtle blue tint to the shadows.
3. Apply a slight-increase in contrast to make the black grill and tires pop.
4. Increase saturation slightly in the yellows/oranges only for the distant traffic lights.
5. Add a soft vignette to draw focus to the center where the car is located.
