# Cinematic Depth of Field & Urban Teal Grade

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9zKpdeviYo` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Depth of Field & Urban Teal Grade](C9zKpdeviYo.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Power Window
- Color Wheels
- Soft Blur

## Parameters

- **blur_radius**: 25.0
- **contrast**: 1.15
- **saturation**: 0.45
- **lift_tint**: Cyan/Blue

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Use a Power Window or Mask to isolate the subject from the background.
2. Apply a Gaussian Blur to the inverted mask to simulate a shallow depth of field.
3. Use the Lift to push shadows toward a teal/blue hue.
4. Use the Midtones and Highlights to maintain warm skin tones (orange).
5. Increase contrast and slightly decrease overall saturation for a moody film look.
