# Cinematic Earth Tones with High Sat Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9ATtzJCXn0` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Earth Tones with High Sat Contrast](C9ATtzJCXn0.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **saturation**: 550
- **contrast**: 1.2
- **lift_offset**: 0.02
- **midtone_tint**: Green/Cyan

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight lift to the blacks to create a matte look.
2. Use the Gamma wheel to pull midtones toward a teal/cyan hue.
3. Use Hue/Saturation curves to shift greens toward a more desaturated olive tone.
4. Apply a Power Window on the yellow car to increase saturation and luminance specifically for the subject.
5. Add subtle vignette to draw focus to the center frame.
6. Add a fine layer of film grain to unify the different shots.
