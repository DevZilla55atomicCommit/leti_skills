# Digital Zoom / Dynamic Zoom Effect

**Source:** Instagram Reel `Cz230RxryiP` (Gimbal_Moves)  
**Page:** Edit | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Digital Zoom / Dynamic Zoom Effect](Cz230RxryiP.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Clip Attributes
- Inspector

## Parameters

- **Dynamic Zoom**: Enabled
- **Zooming**: In
- **Pan/Tilt**: Ease In/Out

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Place the clip on the Timeline
2. Open the Inspector panel and go to the Video tab
3. Toggle the Dynamic Zoom switch ON
4. Adjust the 'Start' frame (green) for the initial wide framing
5. Adjust the 'End' frame (red) to crop into the desired close-up
6. Adjust the Ease Curve to smooth the movement of the zoom
