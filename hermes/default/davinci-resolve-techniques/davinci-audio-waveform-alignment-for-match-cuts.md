# Audio Waveform Alignment for Match Cuts

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9-NoEyOPut` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Edit | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Audio Waveform Alignment for Match Cuts](C9-NoEyOPut.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Timeline
- Audio Waveform

## Parameters

- **zoom_level**: High
- **waveform_visibility**: Enabled

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Place two video clips on separate video tracks (V1 and V2)
2. Zoom in significantly on the timeline to see detailed audio waveforms
3. Identify specific audio peaks or transients in both clips
4. Manually drag the top clip until its waveforms align with the bottom clip
5. Trim the clips at the point of perfect waveform alignment
