# Golden Hour Teal & Orange Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C8c3nGESpWW` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Golden Hour Teal & Orange Contrast](C8c3nGESpWW.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Wheels
- Color Curves
- Power Windows
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **Temperature**: +15
- **Tint**: -5
- **Saturation**: 0.65
- **Gamma_Gain**: +0.1
- **Midtone_Hue**: 0.5 (Orange)

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a primary balancing node to lift shadows slightly to maintain detail in the asphalt.
2. Use the Color Wheels to push the highlights toward orange/yellow and the shadows toward a subtle teal/blue.
3. Use a Power Window on the sky to increase exposure and saturation specifically in the sunset area.
4. Apply a slight S-curve in the Curves tool to increase contrast for a cinematic film look.
5. Add a soft vignette to darken the edges, drawing focus to the central storefront and glowing sky.
