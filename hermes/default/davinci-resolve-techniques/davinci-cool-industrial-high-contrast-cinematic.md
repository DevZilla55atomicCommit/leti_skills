# Cool Industrial High-Contrast Cinematic

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C7tsJIhIdgC` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Cool Industrial High-Contrast Cinematic](C7tsJIhIdgC.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Wheels
- Power Windows
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **exposure**: +0.2
- **contrast**: 1.2
- **saturation**: 35
- **shadow_tint**: Teal/Blue
- **highlight_tint**: Warm/Neutral

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure to recover detail in the stainless steel reflections.
2. Increase contrast to deepen the blacks of the t-shirt.
3. Lower global saturation to achieve a desaturated, professional look.
4. Use the Color Wheels to push a subtle blue/teal tint into the shadows.
5. Apply a soft vignette to focus attention on the hands and the action in the center-frame.
6. Increase midtone highlights slightly to make the texture of the flour and marble.
