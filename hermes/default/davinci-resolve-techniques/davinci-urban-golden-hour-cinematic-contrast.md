# Urban Golden Hour Cinematic Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9KlEA0C5Hv` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Urban Golden Hour Cinematic Contrast](C9KlEA0C5Hv.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Temperature**: +150.0
- **Tint**: -2.0
- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Shadows**: Teal/Blue tint
- **Highlights**: Warm/Orange tint

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a primary exposure correction to balance the de-exposed shadows while keeping highlights bright.
2. Use the Curves tool to create a slight S-curve for increased cinematic contrast.
3. In the Color Wheels, push the Shadows toward a deep teal/cyan and the Highlights toward a warm orange/gold.
4. Apply a Power Window (circular) to the upper building facades to increase exposure and saturation, simulating sunlight hitting.
5. Add a subtle Glow effect with a high threshold and low falloff to soften the highlights on the architectural edges.
