# High-Contrast Natural Low-Angle Editorial

**Source:** Instagram Reel `CfUt5RGjefh` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![High-Contrast Natural Low-Angle Editorial](CfUt5RGjefh.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Window (Masking)
- Color Wheels
- Curves

## Parameters

- **contrast**: 1.2
- **pivot**: 0.5
- **saturation**: 4.5
- **midtone_tint**: Warm/Orange
- **shadow_tint**: Cool/Blue

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure to recover detail in the sky while keeping the foreground shadows deep.
2. Use a Window (Circular) to isolate the subject and slightly increase their exposure/saturation to make them pop.
3. Apply a slight S-curve in the Curves tool to enhance contrast.
4. Use the Color Wheels to add a subtle blue tint to the shadows and a warm gold tint to the highlights/skin tones.
5. Add a subtle vignette to further draw attention to the center where the subject is located.
