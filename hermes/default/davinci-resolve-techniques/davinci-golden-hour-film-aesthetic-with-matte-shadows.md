# Golden Hour Film Aesthetic with Matte Shadows

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C-agQdtvKTu` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Golden Hour Film Aesthetic with Matte Shadows](C-agQdtvKTu.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- Primary Balance
- Curves (Contrast)
- Color Wheels
- Film Grain

## Parameters

- **Temperature**: 6500K
- **Lift Offset**: -0.02
- **Gain Tint**: +0.05
- **Saturation**: 580
- **Grain Intensity**: 0.15

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure to preserve highlights in the sky while keeping detail in shadows.
2. Use the Temperature slider to warm up the image, mimicking golden hour light.
3. In the Curves tool, lift the bottom-left point slightly to create a faded, matte shadow effect.
4. Use the Color Wheels to add a subtle gold/orange tint to the highlights and a slight teal/blue to the shadows.
5. Apply a subtle Film Grain overlay in the final node to emulate cinematic stock.
