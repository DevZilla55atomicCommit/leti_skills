# Urban Muted Matte with Low Angle

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9cco74JKKH` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Urban Muted Matte with Low Angle](C9cco74JKKH.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Contrast Pivot
- Color Wheels
- Sharette

## Parameters

- **lift**: 0.015
- **gamma**: -0.05
- **saturation**: 0.85
- **contrast**: 1.2
- **midtone_tint**: teal/green

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust the Lift slightly upward to create faded/matte black levels.
2. Increase contrast but pull the pivot toward the shadows to preserve highlight detail.
3. Decrease global saturation by 15% to achieve the muted urban look.
4. Apply a subtle teal or green tint to the midtones to complement the olive clothing.
5. Use a qualifier to increase sharpening only on the subject to pop them from the busy background.
