# Vibrant Warm-Contrast Saturation Look

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C-Dl49zOynL` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Vibrant Warm-Contrast Saturation Look](C-Dl49zOynL.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Balance
- HSL Qualifier
- Final Grade

## Parameters

- **Saturation**: 1.2
- **Contrast**: 1.15
- **Midtone Tint**: Warm/Orange
- **Shadow Tint**: Teal/Cool Blue
- **Hue Shift**: Red towards Orange

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Adjust exposure using primary wheels to balance the bright highlights in the sky.
2. Increase contrast slightly to pop the textures of the cobblestones and ivy.
3. Use HSL Qualifier to isolate the red dress and increase its saturation and luminance.
4. Use Hue/Saturation curves to shift the greens of the ivy toward a deeper, more natural forest green.
5. Apply a subtle warm tint (orange) to the highlights and a cool tint (blue) to the shadows for a cinematic complementary effect.
6. Adjust the vignette slightly to draw focus to the center subjects.
