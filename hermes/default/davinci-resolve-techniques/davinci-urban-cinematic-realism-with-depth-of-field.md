# Urban Cinematic Realism with Depth of Field

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C79eoFURekv` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Urban Cinematic Realism with Depth of Field](C79eoFURekv.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Balance
- Color Wheels
- Power Windows
- Vignette

## Parameters

- **Contrast**: 1.15
- **Pivot**: 0.45
- **Gamma Offset**: -0.02
- **Saturate**: 0.85
- **Hue Tint**: Cool (Blue)

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Apply a slight desaturation to achieve a realistic urban street look.
2. Lift the blacks slightly using the curves tool to matte shadows.
3. Use Color Wheels to push shadows toward a subtle blue/teal and highlights toward a neutral/warm tone.
4. Apply a Power Window around the smartphone screen in the bottom shot to increase exposure and sharpness slightly.
5. Add a soft vignette to draw attention to the center where the phone device is located.
