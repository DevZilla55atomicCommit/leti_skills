# Cinematic Teal Fog Atmospheric Color Grading

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C_GV0YCIQUo` (Gimbal_Moves)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Teal Fog Atmospheric Color Grading](C_GV0YCIQUo.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Correction
- Color Wheels
- Qualifiers
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Temperature**: Shifted Blue/Cool
- **Tint**: Shifted Cyan/Green
- **Contrast**: Increased
- **Saturation**: Des-saturated globally, increased selectively for reds

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Import footage and go to the Color page
2. Use Primary Wheels to balance exposure and increase contrast for a deeper moody look
3. Use the Color Wheels to push shadows toward a deep teal/blue and highlights toward a slightly cool or neutral tone
4. Use a Qualifier to select the red taillights and increase their saturation/brightness to ensure they pop
5. Apply a Glow effect or Soften in the highlights to enhance the misty/foggy atmosphere
6. Add a vignette Power Window to draw focus to the car in the center
