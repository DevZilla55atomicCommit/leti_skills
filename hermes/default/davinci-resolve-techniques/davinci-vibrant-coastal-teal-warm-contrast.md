# Vibrant Coastal Teal & Warm Contrast

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C9NUBgxNzWy` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Vibrant Coastal Teal & Warm Contrast](C9NUBgxNzWy.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Primary Wheels
- Curves
- Color Qualifier
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **Saturation**: 65.0
- **Contrast**: 1.2
- **Lift_Hue**: Teal/Cyan
- **Highlights_Tint**: Warm/Yellow
- **Midtone_Saturation**: Vibrant

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Increase contrast and saturation globally to pop the colors of the water and rock.
2. Use the Lift tool to slightly lift the blacks while adding a subtle teal tint to the deep shadows.
3. Apply a Color Qualifier to select the water, shifting the hue toward Cyan and increasing saturation in those areas.
4. Use a Power Window on the cliff face to add a slight warm/orange tint to contrast with the cool water.
5. Use the Curves tool to create a slight S-curve for deeper highlights in the sand and sea foam.
