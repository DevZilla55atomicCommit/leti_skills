# Stabilized Gimbal Tracking Simulation

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C4jkBlivCrs` (Gimbal_Moves)  
**Page:** Edit | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** beginner

![Stabilized Gimbal Tracking Simulation](C4jkBlivCrs.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Video Clip
- Stabilizer

## Parameters

- **Stabilization**: Camera Wide
- **Smoothing**: 50
- **Zoom**: 1.10

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Import the stabilized gimbal footage into the timeline
2. Open the Inspector panel
3. Navigate to the Stabilization tab
4. Select Camera Wide mode
5. Adjust smoothing to remove remaining micro-jitters
6. Apply slight zoom to hide the cropping edges
