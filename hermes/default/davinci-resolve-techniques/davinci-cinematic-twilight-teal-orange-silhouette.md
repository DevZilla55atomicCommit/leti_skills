# Cinematic Twilight Teal & Orange Silhouette

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C89nJD9iJux` (Ideas_for_Shooting_Videos)  
**Page:** Color | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Cinematic Twilight Teal & Orange Silhouette](C89nJD9iJux.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Exposure
- Primary Balance
- Curves
- Color Wheels
- Power Window

## Parameters

- **exposure**: -1.5
- **contrast**: 1.2
- **shadow_tint**: Teal/Blue
- **highlight_tint**: Orange/Gold
- **saturation**: 4.5

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Lower the overall exposure and contrast to underexpose the subject into a silhouette.
2. Use the Lift wheel to push shadows toward a deep teal/blue tone.
3. Use the Gain wheel to push highlights toward a warm orange/gold tone.
4. Apply an S-curve in the Curves tool to deepen the blacks and pop the highlights.
5. Use a Power Window on the subject to further reduce their exposure, ensuring they remain darker than the sky.
6. Add a subtle vignette to draw focus to the center-right and the skyline.
