# Speed Ramp with Motion Blur

**Source:** Instagram Reel `C3wqXKKP7Ft` (Gimbal_Moves)  
**Page:** Edit | **Graph:** serial | **Difficulty:** intermediate

![Speed Ramp with Motion Blur](C3wqXKKP7Ft.gif)

## Node Graph Structure

- Clip Attributes
- Inspector Editor

## Parameters

- **R Curve**: Variable
- **Motion Blur**: Enabled

## Steps to Reproduce in DaVinci Resolve

1. Right-click the clip on the timeline and select Retime Curve
2. Add keyframes on the Retime Curve to vary speed at specific points
3. Increase speed during the camera movement and slow down for the reveal
4. Go to Inspector and enable R Warp
5. Enable Motion Blur and adjust Shutter Angle to smooth the fast transitions
