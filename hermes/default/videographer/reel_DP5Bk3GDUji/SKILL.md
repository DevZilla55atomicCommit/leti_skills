## 🎯 Technique Summary

How do I make my videos both Smooth and Professional? 

1. Eye Fix. 
Meaning you need to lead your viewer's eyes to one spot on the screen through the entire video.

If the focal point keeps jumping around on each, they'll get tired following the video and that is why most TikTok edits feel exhausting.

So go to your editing software, and add a simple guide frame and keep your main elements inside that zone.
