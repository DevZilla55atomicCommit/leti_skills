# Tonga Ride-Hailing Startup Guide

## 1. Regulatory Path
- Primary authority: **Ministry of Infrastructure** (Transport Division)
- Required licenses:
  - Company registration (Foreign Investment Act if non-Tongan ownership)
  - Transport Operator License
  - Public Service Vehicle permits
  - Driver commercial licenses
- Key restrictions:
  - Possible foreign ownership limits in transport
  - Fare regulations may require government approval
  - Sunday commercial transport bans apply
- Process: Submit applications, pay fees, schedule inspections

## 2. Market Assessment
| Metric | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Population** | ~104,000 (70% on Tongatapu) |
| **Addressable market** | ~70K on Tongatapu, ~50-60K annual tourists |
| **Current competition** | Informal 3-wheeler taxis (Bajaj RE/Qute) |
| **Digital readiness** | 90% mobile penetration, cash dominant |
| **Seasonal factors** | Cyclone season (Nov-Apr), tourism peaks |

## 3. Operational Setup
- **Vehicle requirements**: Right-hand drive, suitable for narrow roads
- **Insurance**: Commercial + passenger liability coverage
- **Payment**: Cash dominant; limited card/MPaisa integration
- **Driver recruitment**: Local knowledge essential, background checks required

## 4. Partnerships
- Hotels, airport, cruise ports, tourism operators
- Local businesses for dispatch integration
- Financial institutions for payment processing

## 5. Risks & Mitigation
- **Regulatory delays**: Start licensing process early
- **Cash flow**: Build 6-month runway
- **Political**: Maintain good relations with transport authority
- **Natural disasters**: Cyclone contingency plans

## 6. First Steps
1. Contact Ministry of Infrastructure for license requirements
2. Engage local law firm for company registration
3. Consult Tonga Chamber of Commerce on business setup
4. Talk to existing taxi operators for driver pool insight

> **Note**: All regulatory steps must be completed before launching service.
