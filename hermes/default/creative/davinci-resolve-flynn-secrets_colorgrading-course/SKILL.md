## Overview
@flynn.on.film (Flynn) — Director + Colorist dual-role educator. YouTube tutorials, professional course, narrative-first grading philosophy. "It's time to unveil the secrets" — gateway to deep educational content.

## When to Use
- Seeking structured color grading education
- Want director's perspective on color storytelling
- Need professional workflow (dailies to delivery)
- Learning client communication, project management

## Prerequisites
- Basic DaVinci Resolve knowledge
- Interest in narrative grading (not just "loots")
- Willingness to invest in education (course) or time (YouTube)
