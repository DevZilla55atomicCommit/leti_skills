## Steps

1. Navigate to the Edit Page in DaVinci Resolve.
2. Select the clip you want to move in the timeline.
3. Press **Ctrl + Shift + Up/Down** (Windows) or **Cmd + Shift + Up/Down** (Mac).
4. The selected clip will swap positions with the adjacent clip in the direction pressed.
5. Repeat as needed to reorder clips quickly without manual dragging.
