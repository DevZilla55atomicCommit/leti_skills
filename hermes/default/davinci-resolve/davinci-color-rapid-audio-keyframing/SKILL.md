## Steps

1. Navigate to the Edit page in DaVinci Resolve.
2. Expand the audio track to see the volume line.
3. Hold **Option** (Mac) or **Alt** (Windows) and click on the volume line where you want to add a keyframe.
4. The keyframe is created instantly without opening menus.
5. Drag the keyframe up/down to adjust volume at that point.
6. Drag left/right to adjust timing.
