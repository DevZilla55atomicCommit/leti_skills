## Steps

1. Add a Serial Primary Color node to perform a basic exposure balance, slightly underexposing the image to enhance the mood.
2. Add a Curves node and drop the overall saturation, specifically targeting the greens to make them less 'vibrant'.
3. Use a Qualifier to select the yellow/orange autumn leaves; increase their saturation and luminance to create a color contrast pop against the cool background.
4. Apply a Power Window (Linear Gradient) on the sky/mountain area; decrease the exposure and add a blue tint to create a sense of atmospheric haze and distance.
5. Use the Teal and Orange color balance by pushing shadows into teal and highlights/midtones into a warm gold/orange tone.
6. Add a soft vignette using a circular window to frame the road and focus the viewer's attention on the center leading lines.
