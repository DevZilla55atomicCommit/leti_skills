# Videography Storyboard & Photography Shot List Templates

This skill centralizes the templates we created for videography storyboarding and photography shot listing.

## Templates Overview

| Template | Description | Vault Path |
|----------|-------------|------------|
| **Videography Storyboard** | Temporal sequence with duration, camera moves, audio cues, continuity checks | `/Users/alfredkamisese/TamaZila Obsidian Vault/Machine/Templates/Videography Storyboard.md` |
| **Photography Shot List** | Static shot checklist with technical settings, lighting, wardrobe, post‑production notes | `/Users/alfredkamisese/TamaZila Obsidian Vault/Machine/Templates/Photography Shot List.md` |

## How to Access

Load this skill and retrieve the templates via `skill_view` or copy them directly into your project.

## Usage Tips

- Use the videography storyboard when planning video shoots that require timing, motion, and audio.
- Use the photography shot list for static captures, focusing on composition, lighting, and technical exposure.
- Both templates include continuity checklists to ensure consistency across shots.
