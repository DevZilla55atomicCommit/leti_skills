# Hawaiian BBQ Poster Prompt Guide

## Branding & Typography
- Restaurant name: "KAILAHI BBQ" in bold, bubbly surf-style font.
- Gradient: coral/orange/yellow with teal outline.
- Placement: top center, maximum legibility at distance.

## Visual Elements
- Setting: Sunset beach, palm trees framing, string lights overhead.
- Family: Multi-generational Hawaiian family (grandparents, parents, kids) laughing around a wooden picnic table.
- Decor: Plumeria, hibiscus, bird of paradise flowers; tiki torches.

## Food Items to Include
- Kalua pig (slow-roasted pork) with cabbage.
- Huli huli chicken (grilled chicken skewers) with char marks.
- Grilled mahi-mahi with mango salsa.
- Portuguese sausage slices.
- Haupia (coconut pudding) dessert.
- Poi, white rice, macaroni salad, grilled pineapple rings, corn on the cob.

## Color Palette
- Warm tones: coral, golden yellow, teal, palm green, sunset orange.
- High saturation for vibrant, inviting feel.
- Contrast: teal/orange color grade for cinematic look.

## Technical Specs
- Aspect ratio: 1024x1536 (portrait).
- Lighting: golden hour rim lighting, warm highlights.
- Camera: Sony A7IV, 35mm f/1.4 GM lens.
- Film emulation: Kodak Portra 400.
- Depth of field: shallow, creamy bokeh background.
- Style: Editorial advertising, vibrant, joyful, professional commercial quality.
